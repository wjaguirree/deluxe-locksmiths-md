Lost Car Key Replacement in Clayton - common questions

How do you make a new car key in Clayton if I don't have the old one?

We identify the vehicle, check the lock or ignition setup, and use the right method to originate a new key. Some cars need a cut key plus programming. Others need a different approach. If the vehicle is parked and accessible, we can handle the work there instead of asking you to bring it in.

What should I have ready before I call about my lost car key?

Have the vehicle year, make, and model, plus the location of the car in Clayton. It also helps to have proof that the vehicle belongs to you. If you know whether the key had a chip, remote buttons, or push-start, tell us that too. That keeps the job on the right track.

Can a lost key turn into a safety problem if someone finds it near Clayton?

Yes, especially if the missing key had identifying info or was lost with vehicle paperwork. In that case, we may recommend rekeying or another change so the old key no longer works. If the key is truly gone and there's a concern about theft, we'll talk through the safest option for the car.

Should I repair the lock, rekey it, or replace it after losing my car key?

If the lock and ignition still work well, repair may be enough. If you want the old key code changed, rekeying is the better choice. If the lock is damaged, the key broke inside it, or the system is too worn to trust, replacement is usually the right call. We look at the actual condition before deciding.

What if my car key was lost and the ignition key turns hard or sticks?

That usually means there's wear in the cylinder as well as a key problem. We can inspect the lock, see whether it can be repaired, and decide if a fresh key will solve it or if the cylinder needs to be rekeyed or replaced. We don't guess when the hardware is already telling us something is wrong.
