Lost Car Key Replacement in Glasgow - common questions

How does lost car key replacement work if I have no key left in Glasgow?

We come to the vehicle, identify the key type, and make a new key that matches the car. If the vehicle uses a chip key or smart key, we also handle programming. When nothing is left to copy, the work starts from the car itself, not from a spare.

What should I have ready when you come replace my car key?

Have your registration and a photo ID ready, and if the car is yours but someone else is present, make sure they can help confirm it. If you have any remaining fobs, broken pieces, or a spare key at home, bring that information too. It can help us identify the right replacement.

My car unlocks but won't start. Is that still a lost key problem?

Not always. That can be a dead fob battery, a damaged chip, or a programming issue instead of a fully lost key. We check the symptoms first so we don't replace more than the vehicle actually needs. If there's no usable key left at all, then it's a full replacement job.

Should I replace my lost key with a regular key or a fob?

Use whatever the vehicle was designed to accept. A plain key works on some older vehicles, but many newer ones need a chip key or smart key to start. We match the replacement to the car's system, not just to what seems simpler. That avoids a key that unlocks doors but won't run the engine.

Can you make a new car key in a parking lot near Glasgow Park if I'm locked out?

Yes, if the vehicle and the key system allow on-site work. A parking lot is a normal place for this kind of call. We verify the vehicle, cut or originate the key, and program it when needed. If the car has a system that requires dealer-only equipment, we'll tell you that plainly.
