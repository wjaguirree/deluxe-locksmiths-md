Safe Opening Service in Havre de Grace - common questions

What do you do when a safe in Havre de Grace won't open at all?

We start by identifying the type of safe and the failure point, then use the least invasive method that fits the lock. That might mean dialing, bypassing a keypad issue, addressing a broken key, or opening a mechanical lockout. Afterward, we check whether the lock, boltwork, or keypad needs repair or replacement.

What should I have ready before you come to my house or shop?

If you can, have the safe's make, model, and any key, combination note, or owner paperwork ready. Tell us what happened right before it locked up and whether anyone else has used it lately. If it's in an office, make sure the person who can authorize the opening is available by phone or on site.

Could trying to open the safe myself make the problem worse?

Yes. Forcing the handle, prying the door, drilling blindly, or spraying the wrong product into the lock can damage the internals and make a clean opening harder. If the safe has valuables, records, or a firearm inside, leave it shut and let us handle the opening with the right tools and method.

Should I try the keypad again or wait if the battery seems dead?

If the keypad is dim, unresponsive, or acting irregularly, stop repeated attempts and avoid forcing the handle. A dead battery may be the whole issue, but repeated tries can drain what's left or mask a deeper fault. If there's an external battery option or override key and you know it's correct, use only the method the safe was designed for.

My safe is old and the dial feels gritty. Can it still be opened without damaging it?

Often, yes, but old safes can have worn parts, hardened grease, or bent linkages that change how we work them. We'll inspect the condition first and choose the approach that best protects the safe body and the contents.
