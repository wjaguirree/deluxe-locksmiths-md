Car Lockouts in Camden - common questions

How do you get into a locked car without damaging the door?

We use non-destructive entry methods chosen for the vehicle and lock style. The point is to unlock the car without harming the glass, paint, weather seal, or trim. If the lock or fob issue suggests a deeper problem, we can explain that after the door is open.

What should I have ready when I call about a car lockout in Camden?

Have the vehicle make, model, color, and exact location ready, along with proof that the car is yours or you're allowed to access it. If the keys are visible inside, mention that too. That helps avoid delays and tells us what kind of entry method may be needed.

Can trying to open the car myself make things worse?

Yes. Pry tools, coat hangers, or forcing the door can bend the frame, scratch the paint, or damage the lock and side glass. That often turns a lockout into a repair job. It's better to stop once you know the keys are locked inside and let it be opened the right way.

If my key fob stopped working, do I need a lockout service or a repair?

If the doors won't unlock because the fob is dead or acting up, a lockout service may still be the first step to get you back in. After that, the issue may be battery, fob, or vehicle-related. A proper check helps you decide whether the real fix is the key, the lock, or both.

What if the car is locked and the engine is running?

That's an edge case where you want the door opened carefully and the situation handled right away. Tell us the engine status when you call. Depending on the vehicle, the locking system and safety features can change how entry has to be done.
