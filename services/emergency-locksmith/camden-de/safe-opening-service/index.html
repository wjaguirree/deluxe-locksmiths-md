Safe Opening Service in Camden - common questions

How do you open a safe in Camden if the combination is lost?

We start by identifying the safe type and lock style, then use the least invasive opening method that fits the situation. Some safes can be opened through the lock itself, while others need controlled mechanical access. The goal is to get the door open while preserving the safe as much as possible.

What should I have ready before you come out to my house or office?

If you have the brand name, model number, old paperwork, or a photo of the safe door and keypad, that helps. It's also useful to know whether it's a dial, keypad, or key-lock safe. If the safe is bolted down or hidden in furniture, mention that so the setup is clear.

Can trying the wrong code damage the safe or lock?

It can, especially if the keypad is already failing or the lock is under strain. Repeated forcing can make a small problem worse and sometimes leaves the handle stuck. If the safe is acting up, stop working the mechanism and describe exactly what it does when you try to open it.

Should I choose opening only, or opening and service too?

If the safe just needs access once, opening may be enough. If the dial is rough, the keypad is unreliable, or the lock has been acting strange, service makes more sense so the same problem doesn't keep coming back. We can talk through which route fits the condition of the safe.

What if the safe is an old home safe or a small office safe with no brand name visible?

That happens all the time. A lack of visible branding doesn't stop the job, but it can change the opening method. A clear photo of the front, hinges, handle, and lock area is helpful. If the safe is very old, we may need to take a more careful, measured approach.
