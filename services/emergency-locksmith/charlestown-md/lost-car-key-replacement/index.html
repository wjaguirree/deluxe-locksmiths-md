Lost Car Key Replacement in Charlestown - common questions

What happens when I've lost every car key in Charlestown and there's nothing to copy?

We identify the vehicle, confirm ownership, and make the key on site from scratch. That can mean cutting a new blade, programming a chip key, or setting up a fob so the car accepts it. There's no need for an existing key when the system allows on-site origin and programming.

What should I have ready before you come out for a lost car key job?

Have the vehicle make, model, year, and proof that it's yours. If you know whether the car uses a metal key, transponder key, or push-button fob, that helps too. If the car has been sitting near a marina or tucked behind another vehicle, make sure there's enough access to work on the driver's side and the ignition area.

Can a new key be made if my car is old but the locks are worn out?

Usually, yes, but worn locks can affect how well a newly cut key turns. We can still work from the vehicle's lock or key code when the system allows it. If the lock is badly worn, we'll let you know whether the key alone will solve it or whether the lock itself needs attention later.

Should I choose a basic key or a fob replacement for my Charlestown vehicle?

That depends on what the car was built to use. Some older vehicles only need a cut key, while many newer ones require a programmed chip or fob to start the engine. If you're not sure, we can identify the right type from the vehicle itself and make the replacement that matches its system.

What if my car is parked on a narrow street or in a tight waterfront spot?

That's usually workable as long as we can get to the vehicle and the driver's door, ignition, or key slot. A tight parking space doesn't change the method; it just means we work carefully around the car where it sits. If the vehicle can't be accessed safely, we'll talk through the next practical step.
