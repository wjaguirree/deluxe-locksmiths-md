If I lost every key to my car in Edgewood, what do you need from me to make a new one?

Have the car's make, model, and year ready, along with proof that you're the owner or authorized driver. If the registration or title is in the vehicle, tell us that before we head out. The vehicle itself matters too, since the lock and key system can vary even within the same model line.

How does a lost key replacement work when the car is parked in an apartment lot in Edgewood?

We come to the car, identify the key type, cut a new key, and program it if the vehicle uses a chip or smart fob. Doing it at the car avoids towing and avoids trying to move a vehicle that cannot be started. We also test the doors and ignition before wrapping up.

Will a new key still work if my old one was lost near the Edgewood Area of APG?

Yes, the lost key's location doesn't matter. What matters is the vehicle's lock and security system. We can make a replacement from the car itself and, when needed, program it so the missing key no longer controls the vehicle.

Should I ask for a basic key or a remote key for my car in Edgewood?

What if my car was sold with only one key and now that one is gone?
