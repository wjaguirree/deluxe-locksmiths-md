Safe Opening Service in Felton - common questions

How do you open a safe in Felton if the combination is gone?

We identify the safe type first, then use the least invasive opening method that fits that model. Some safes can be opened through the lock itself, while others need a different approach. After that, we can talk through repair, reset, or replacement based on the condition of the lock and the cabinet.

What should I have ready before you come to my house or office?

If you have the brand name, model number, old paperwork, or even a photo of the safe face and lock, that helps. It's also useful to know whether it's a dial, keypad, or key-operated safe, and whether anything happened right before it stopped opening. That gives us a better starting point.

Can opening the safe damage what's inside?

Our goal is to open the safe without harming the contents, but that depends on the safe design and what failed. We work carefully, and if the safe has already been forced or is badly stuck, we'll explain the risk before going further. A damaged lock can sometimes be opened cleanly, but not every safe is the same.

Should I repair the safe, rekey it, or replace it after it's opened?

Repair makes sense when the lock or latch is worn but the safe itself is still solid. Rekeying or resetting works when the access code or key setup is the issue. Replacement is usually the choice when the safe has major damage, repeated failures, or parts that can't be trusted anymore.

What if my safe is at a campsite or out on a farm road near Felton?

We handle on-site work, so the location matters less than getting the details right. Let us know what kind of safe it is, where it's mounted, and whether there's power for a keypad model. If access is awkward, we'll plan the job around the space and the equipment needed to service it properly.
