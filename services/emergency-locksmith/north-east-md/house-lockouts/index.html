If my deadbolt is stuck on a house in North East, do you try to open it without replacing the lock first?

Yes. We start by checking the cylinder, latch, and alignment to see whether the lock can be opened and kept in service. If the parts are worn or broken, we'll explain that before changing anything. The aim is to get the door open cleanly and leave you with a lock that works the way it should.

What should I have ready when I call about a home lockout near Elk Neck State Park?

Have the exact address, the kind of door you're dealing with, and a quick description of the lock if you can see it. If there's a key broken in the cylinder, a loose knob, or a deadbolt that won't retract, mention that. If you have another way to prove you live there, keep it handy.

Can trying to force the key make the lock worse?

It can. A bent key, a jammed deadbolt, or a gritty cylinder can get damaged if pressure is pushed too hard. That can leave broken key pieces inside or bend the latch out of line. If the key isn't turning normally, stop using it and let us work on the hardware before more parts fail.

Is it better to rekey the lock or replace the whole deadbolt after a lockout?

If the lock is sound and the key issue is the main problem, rekeying may be enough. If the cylinder is worn, the bolt sticks, or the knob has play in it, replacement is usually the better fix. We look at the condition of the hardware first and recommend the option that makes the door secure again.

What if my front door at a rental cottage has a smart lock or keypad and the battery died?

That's a common lockout. Some electronic locks have a backup keyway or an external battery contact, while others need the trim removed and the hardware reset. If the lock is still operating correctly once power is restored, we can usually get it open and check whether the keypad, latch, or deadbolt needs attention.
