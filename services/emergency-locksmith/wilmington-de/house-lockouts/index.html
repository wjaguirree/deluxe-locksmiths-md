How do you open a locked house door without tearing it up?

What should I have ready when I call about my Wilmington house lockout?

Have the exact address, your name, and a way to show you live there or are allowed inside. If it's an apartment, tell us the unit number and whether there are building access rules. If there's a deadbolt, smart lock, or broken key involved, mention that too so we know what we're walking into.

Can you still help if my key snapped off in the lock?

Yes, that's a common lockout problem. If part of the key is visible, don't keep poking at it or try to glue it. That can push the piece deeper. Tell us whether the deadbolt is engaged and whether the door is stuck, because that affects how we open it and whether the lock needs repair afterward.
