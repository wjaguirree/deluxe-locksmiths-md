Broken Key Extraction in Wilmington - common questions

How do you get a broken key out without damaging the lock?

We start by checking how much of the blade is still visible and whether the cylinder is sitting straight. Then we use the right extraction tools for that lock or ignition, so we can pull the piece without chewing up the pins, wafers, or keyway. After that, we test the lock and let you know if the key still needs to be replaced.

What should I have ready if my key snapped in my front door near Rodney Square?

Have proof that you live there or that you're allowed into the property, plus any working spare key if you have one. If it's a vehicle, the make, model, and year help us bring the right tools. Don't keep trying to turn the broken piece, because that can push it farther in and make the lock harder to save.

Is it risky to leave a broken key in the lock until morning?

It can be. If the broken piece is still in place, the lock may not secure fully, and a door that doesn't latch or lock right can become a security problem. On a car, you may also end up stranded if the key shifts or falls deeper. If the lock is partway engaged, it's better to have it handled before it becomes a bigger repair.

Should I try to extract the broken key or replace the lock?

If the lock is otherwise healthy, extraction and a replacement key are often the first step. If the cylinder is worn, the key broke because the lock was already binding, or the mechanism feels rough after the piece comes out, replacement may be the better choice. We can look at both options and tell you which one makes sense for that hardware.

What if my car key broke off in the ignition and part of it is still inside?

That can usually still be handled, but the approach depends on the vehicle and how deeply the blade broke. We extract the piece first, then check whether the ignition cylinder or the key itself caused the failure. If the blade snapped because the key was worn, we can help you get back to a key that turns cleanly instead of forcing the same problem again.
