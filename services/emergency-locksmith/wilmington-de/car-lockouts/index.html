Car Lockouts in Wilmington - common questions

How do you open my car without bending the door or scratching the paint?

We use non-destructive entry methods matched to the vehicle's lock style. That lets us work at the lock or latch without prying the door open. The aim is to leave the paint, glass, seals, and trim untouched and get the vehicle open in a clean way.

What should I have ready when I call about a lockout in Wilmington?

Have the vehicle make, model, color, and exact location ready. If the keys are visible inside, say that too. We may also need to confirm that you're allowed to access the vehicle, especially if it's parked in a lot, garage, or shared building area.

Can a lockout job damage my alarm or window switches?

It can if the wrong method is used. Forcing the door or working a tool through the wrong spot can trigger the alarm or damage wiring inside the door. We avoid that by choosing the entry point and method carefully, based on the vehicle and the lock setup.

Should I try the spare key or ask for car lockout help first?

If you have a spare key close by, that's usually the simplest option. If the spare isn't available or the car won't respond to it, a lockout service is the better move. We can also tell you if the issue sounds more like a dead battery than a true lockout.

What if my keys are locked in the trunk or the car is a push-button start?

Both come up often. Trunk lockouts and push-button vehicles can take a different approach than a standard keyed door. We work from the vehicle's actual setup, not a one-method-fits-all trick, and we'll tell you if the situation needs a different kind of automotive service.
