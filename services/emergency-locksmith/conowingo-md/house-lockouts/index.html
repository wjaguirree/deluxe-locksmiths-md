How do you open a locked house in Conowingo without breaking the door?

We start by looking at the lock, the door, and the frame so we can choose the cleanest way in. A lot depends on whether it's a simple latch issue or a deadbolt problem. Our goal is to open the door with as little disturbance as possible and make sure it closes and locks normally afterward.

What should I have ready when you come to my house lockout in Conowingo?

Have a way to confirm the address and some proof that you live there, since we need to protect the homeowner and the property. If a door has been sticking, warped, or hard to close, it helps to mention that up front. If anyone is inside, let us know before we start.

I'm worried the lock is jammed, not just locked. Can you tell the difference at the door?

Yes. Sometimes a door is truly locked, and sometimes the latch, bolt, or frame is the real issue. We inspect what we can before we start forcing anything. If the lock is damaged or the door is shifted in the frame, we'll handle it in a way that avoids making the problem worse.

Should I try a spare key or force the knob if I'm locked out near the dam?

A spare key is always better than forcing the door. Prying, twisting the knob hard, or using tools the wrong way can damage the lock, the trim, or the frame. If there's a spare nearby, that may solve it. If not, we can open it properly and check the lock afterward.

What if my house key is inside, but my dog is loose in the yard or the house?
