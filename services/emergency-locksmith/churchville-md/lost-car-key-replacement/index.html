Lost Car Key Replacement in Churchville - common questions

If I lost every key to my car in Churchville, what do you actually do?

We identify the vehicle, cut a new key, and program it on site when the system requires it. If there's no key left to copy, that's not a problem. The goal is to make a working key at the car so the vehicle can be opened and started again.

What should I have ready before you come out to my car?

Have the vehicle year, make, model, and, if possible, the VIN. Keep proof of ownership handy and make sure you can show where the car is parked. If there's a spare at home or with a family member, mention that too. Those details help avoid delays once we get there.

I'm worried the car alarm or anti-theft system will make this harder. Is that a problem?

Sometimes it adds a step, especially on newer cars or vehicles with chipped keys. That doesn't mean the job can't be done. We'll work with the system the car has and make sure the new key is recognized before we finish.

Should I try to break into the car or remove the ignition myself before you arrive?

No. If you pry on the door, damage the lock, or tamper with the ignition, you can turn a key replacement into a lock or repair issue too. If the vehicle is safe where it sits, leave it alone and let us handle the key work.

What if the key broke off and I can't find the rest of it at my house in Churchville?

That's still workable in many cases. A broken key doesn't always mean the vehicle needs a full lock replacement. If the ignition or door lock is damaged, we'll see that once we're there and explain the next step plainly.
