Locked out, or dealing with a key that broke off in the lock, we start by figuring out what still works and what doesn't. If the hardware is solid, a repair or broken key extraction may be enough. Emergency locksmith in Aberdeen - common questions

If I'm locked out near Aberdeen Proving Ground, do you try the lock first or go straight to replacing it?

We start with the least disruptive option that fits the lock and the situation. If the hardware is sound, we may be able to open it, repair it, or rekey it. We only replace parts when they're damaged, worn out, or no longer dependable.

What should I have ready if my car key is lost at a lot in Aberdeen?

Have your vehicle information ready, along with proof that it's your car. If you have any spare key, key fob, or paperwork that helps identify the vehicle, that can help us choose the right replacement path. For newer vehicles, the type of key matters a lot.

Can a broken key in my front door turn into a bigger problem if I try to pull it out myself?

Yes. Pushing or prying at the fragment can damage the cylinder or make the break harder to remove. We use extraction methods that protect the lock when possible. If the lock is already damaged, we'll tell you whether repair or replacement makes more sense.

Should I rekey the house I just moved into in Aberdeen, or replace the locks?

If the locks are in good shape, rekeying is often the cleaner choice because it changes who can open them without changing the whole lock. If the hardware is worn, mismatched, or not working smoothly, replacement may be the better long-term fix.

My safe won't open, but I don't want it damaged. What can you do?

We first look at the type of safe and the kind of failure you're dealing with. Some safes can be opened with careful service and little or no damage, while others may need more invasive work. If destruction is even a possibility, we'll say so before we proceed.
