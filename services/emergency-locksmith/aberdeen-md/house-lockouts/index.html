House Lockouts in Aberdeen - common questions

What should I check before I call for a house lockout in Aberdeen?

Check every exterior door, including the garage entry if it leads into the house. See whether a window or back door was left unlocked, and ask anyone with a spare if they're nearby. If you have a smart lock or keypad, note any warning lights, low battery signs, or error messages before you call.

Will opening my locked house damage the door or the lock?

It shouldn't if the right method is used. The goal is to open the door with the least disturbance possible, then test the latch and deadbolt after entry. If the lock is already worn, the key is bent, or the door is out of alignment, the hardware may need repair after the lockout is handled.

I'm locked out near Aberdeen Proving Ground. What should I have ready when help gets there?

Have a photo ID and something that shows you're supposed to be there, such as mail, a lease, or a utility bill if it applies. If you're waiting outside a gate or coming from work, say that clearly. It helps to know whether the lock is a deadbolt, knob lock, keypad, or smart lock.

If my key turns but the door still won't open, is that still a lockout?

Yes. That can happen when the latch sticks, the deadbolt isn't fully retracting, or the door is misaligned in the frame. Don't force the handle hard, because that can snap the key or break the lock. A locksmith can open it and then check whether the hardware needs adjustment.

What if the key is broken off inside the lock when I'm locked out?

Tell the locksmith right away and don't keep turning the remaining piece. A broken key can block the cylinder and make the lock harder to open. In some cases the broken piece can be removed and the lock saved. In others, the cylinder may need to be replaced before the door can be secured properly again.
