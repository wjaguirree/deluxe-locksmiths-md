Safe Opening Service in Aberdeen - common questions

How does safe opening usually work for a home safe in Aberdeen?

We start by identifying the safe, the lock type, and the problem you're seeing. Then we choose the least destructive method that fits that safe. Some opens are straightforward access jobs. Others need more careful work because the lock or bolt is damaged. After opening, we can talk through repair options if the safe needs service.

What should I have ready when I call about a safe on Beards Hill Road?

If you know the brand, model, or serial number, that helps. It also helps to know whether it's a dial safe, keypad safe, or a simple key lock. If you can, tell us whether the safe is at home or at a business and whether anything has been tried already. That gives a clearer picture of the job.

Can trying the wrong code damage the safe or lock?

It can, depending on the safe. Repeated wrong attempts may trigger lockout features or add wear to the keypad, dial, or boltwork. For some safes, forcing the handle or turning the dial hard is what causes the bigger problem. If the safe is resisting, it's better to stop and have it looked at before more parts get damaged.

Should I ask for opening only, or open and repair if the safe is acting up?

If the safe has been hard to turn, the keypad has been unreliable, or the handle feels off, it makes sense to mention that when you call. Opening only may solve the access problem for now, but a worn lock or failing keypad can leave you stuck again. In those cases, repair or reworking the lock is usually the better next step.

What if the safe is built into a wall or set into office furniture?

That's common, and it changes the job. Built-in safes can be harder to reach, and there may be less room to work around the lock or door. Tell us where it's mounted and what access looks like. In some cases we can open it in place. In others, the setup determines how the opening has to be handled.
