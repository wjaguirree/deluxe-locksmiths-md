How do you get a house open without damaging the door?

We start by identifying the lock type and the safest way to open it. On many homes, that means using methods that protect the door, frame, and trim. If the hardware is worn or misaligned, we'll account for that while opening it and make sure the door still latches properly when we're done.

What should I have ready when I'm locked out of my home in Hockessin?

Have a way to confirm the address, any ID you can reach, and a phone with enough charge to stay in contact. If there's a side door, garage entry, or gate that matters to the job, mention it right away. That helps us choose the right approach and avoid unnecessary damage.

Can a lockout turn into a bigger problem with older doors?

Yes. Older doors in the area sometimes have shifted frames, sticky latches, or hardware that has been painted, patched, or replaced over time. If we see that, we'll open the door carefully and point out what's affecting the lock so the same issue doesn't trap you again.

Should I ask for rekeying or just get the door open first?

If you're locked out, the first step is getting access back without damaging the door. After that, we can talk through whether rekeying makes sense, especially if a key was lost or copied and you want better control over who can get in. The right choice depends on the hardware and your situation.

What if my lockout is really at a gate or detached garage instead of the front door?
