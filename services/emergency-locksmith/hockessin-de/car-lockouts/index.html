How do you open my car without scratching the door in Hockessin?

We use non-destructive entry tools and choose the method based on the vehicle's lock type, trim, and latch design. If the car has electronic locks, we also check whether the issue is the door hardware or the vehicle's power system.

What should I have ready before I call about my locked car?

Have the vehicle make, model, and year ready, and tell us whether the key is inside, missing, or broken. If the car is in a driveway, lot, or behind a gate in Hockessin, that helps too. Can trying to open the door myself damage the lock or airbag area?

It can. Modern doors hide wiring, side-impact airbags, and delicate trim behind the panel. Prying at the wrong point can bend the frame, mar the paint, or harm the lock linkage. We use tools made for vehicle entry so the door, glass, and interior parts stay protected while we work.

We can open the car and help identify whether the problem is the fob battery, the vehicle battery, or the lock hardware itself.
