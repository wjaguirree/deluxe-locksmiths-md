Emergency locksmith in Hockessin - common questions

I'm locked out of my house in Hockessin. What should I do before we call?

Stay calm and check every legal way back in, including a side door, garage entry, or a spare key you already have access to. Don't force the lock or try to slip a tool in the door. If the key broke, keep the pieces if you can do so safely. That helps us decide whether extraction, rekeying, or entry is the right next step.

My car key snapped off near Lancaster Pike. Should I try to pull it out myself?

Usually no. Picking at the broken piece can push it deeper or damage the cylinder. If part of the key is visible, leave it alone and let us handle the extraction. If you have a spare, keep it ready. We can also help with replacement when the original key can't be saved.

I rent in Hockessin and I'm locked out. Do I need my landlord there?

It depends on the property and the situation. For a standard tenant lockout, we need to verify that you're allowed to request entry. If the lock needs to be changed or there's a dispute about access, the landlord or manager may need to be involved. It's better to sort that out before anyone starts drilling or replacing parts.

My gate won't release at my place near the Ashland Nature Center. Is it better to force it open or wait?

Wait. Forcing a stuck gate can bend the latch, twist the hinges, or damage the lock body. If the gate controls access to a driveway or outbuilding, the problem may be a misaligned latch, a worn lock, or a key issue. Tell us what's happening, and we'll work the hardware the right way.

Can you open a safe if the dial is jammed or the key is missing?

In many cases, yes, but the approach depends on the safe type and whether it's a mechanical or keyed model. Don't keep spinning a stuck dial or drilling random spots. That can make the safe harder to service and may damage what's inside. Give us the brand and model if you know it, and we'll talk through the safest option.
