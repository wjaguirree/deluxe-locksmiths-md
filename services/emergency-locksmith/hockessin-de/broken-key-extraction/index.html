Broken Key Extraction in Hockessin - common questions

How do you get a broken key out of a front door lock in Hockessin?

We look at how the key broke, how much is exposed, and whether the cylinder is still lined up. If it can be removed cleanly, we use proper extraction tools instead of forcing the lock. After that, we check the lock and test it with a working key so you know whether the hardware is still usable.

What should I have ready before you come out to a broken key call?

Have the door, gate, or lock area clear so we can work without extra delay. If there's another key on site, keep it handy. It also helps to know whether the key broke in a house lock, a gate, a padlock, or a car door, since each one is handled a little differently.

Can a broken key damage a high-security lock in Hockessin?

It can if someone keeps trying to turn it or starts poking at the cylinder with the wrong tool. High-security hardware is common around here, and it should be treated carefully. We work to protect the lock body and the keyway, but if the lock was already worn or the key was bent, the cylinder may still need attention.

Should I replace the lock after the key snaps, or can it stay?

That depends on why it broke. If the key was just stressed once, the lock may be fine after extraction. If the cylinder is rough, the key was already hard to turn, or the same thing has happened before, replacement may make more sense. We can look at both options and tell you what we see.

What if my key broke off in a gate lock or outbuilding lock near Ashland Nature Center?

That's a common call on properties with detached buildings or gates. Those locks often sit exposed to weather and can bind more than people expect. We can remove the broken piece and check whether the issue is just the key or whether the lock itself is sticking from age, dirt, or wear.
