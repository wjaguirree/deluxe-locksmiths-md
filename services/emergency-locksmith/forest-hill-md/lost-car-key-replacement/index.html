If that chip is missing, damaged, or no longer recognized, the car may turn over and still refuse to start, or the remote may stop working even though the blade part still fits the door. Lost Car Key Replacement in Forest Hill - common questions

How does lost car key replacement work if I have no key left in Forest Hill?

We identify the vehicle, confirm ownership, and work from the car itself to make a new key. If the car uses a chip or fob, we also program it so the vehicle recognizes it. The goal is to get you a key that starts the car and matches its security system.

What should I have ready before you come out for my car key?

Have the vehicle's make, model, year, and VIN if you can get it. Proof that the car is yours is important too. If there's any detail about the key style, like push-to-start, transponder, or remote fob, mention that up front so the right equipment comes with the job.

Can a lost key replacement damage my car or mess up the security system?

Not when it's done the right way. The main risk is trying the wrong key type or forcing a shortcut on a vehicle that needs programming. We match the key to the car and use the proper process so the new key works without upsetting the immobilizer or lock system.

Should I make a spare key at the same time, or is one new key enough?

One new key will get you back on the road, but a spare is smart if you're down to zero. If the car still has a working key after the emergency is handled, making another one can save you from going through the same problem again if one gets misplaced or damaged.

What if my lost key was a push-to-start fob and not a regular key?

That's a different job, but it's still doable in the field on many vehicles. We need the right fob for the system, then it has to be programmed to the car. The exact steps depend on the make and model, so the vehicle information matters even more in that case.
