What actually happens is more controlled than most people expect. Broken Key Extraction in Forest Hill - common questions

If my key broke off in the front door lock in Forest Hill, what do you actually do first?

We look at how much of the blade is still inside and whether the lock is under tension. Then we remove the broken piece without pushing it deeper. After that, we test the cylinder and check whether the lock still turns cleanly. If the lock is damaged or binding, extraction alone may not solve the problem.

Do I need to bring the broken key, or is the lock enough for you to work on?

Bring whatever pieces you have, but the lock is what matters most. If the break happened at your house or vehicle in Forest Hill, we can work from the cylinder itself. A matching spare key can help us compare wear and see whether the broken one was already badly cut or worn down.

Could trying to pull the broken piece out myself make things worse?

Yes. Tweezers, glue, or a makeshift tool can push the fragment deeper, scratch the keyway, or bend parts inside the lock. That can turn a simple extraction into a lock repair. If the piece is visible, it still may be better to leave it alone until a locksmith can remove it cleanly.

How do I know if I need broken key extraction or a full lock repair?

If the key snapped but the lock still works smoothly after the piece is removed, extraction may be enough. If the lock was hard to turn before the break, or if the key keeps sticking, the cylinder may be worn or misaligned. In that case, repair or replacement may be the better option.

What if the key broke in my car ignition and the door key still works?

That can happen. A door cylinder and an ignition cylinder don't wear the same way, even on the same vehicle. We can extract the broken piece from the ignition and check whether the ignition itself is worn or damaged. A working door key doesn't always mean the ignition is still in good shape.
