Lost Car Key Replacement in New Castle - common questions

If I lost every key to my car in New Castle, how do you make a new one?

We work from the vehicle itself. After we confirm ownership, we identify the key type, cut a new key, and program it when the car requires programming. No original key has to be available. If the car uses a transponder, smart key, or push-button system, we match the replacement to that vehicle.

What should I have ready before you come to my car?

Have your driver's license and vehicle registration ready if you can. It also helps to know the car's year, make, and model, plus where the vehicle is parked. If there's a spare key at home, tell us. The more accurate the information, the smoother the job usually goes.

I'm worried the dealer will make me tow the car. Is that always necessary?

Not always. Many lost-key jobs can be handled where the car is parked, depending on the vehicle and key system. Some newer models are more involved, but a tow is not automatically required. We'll look at the car first and tell you what the vehicle actually needs.

Should I try to open the car or ignition myself while I wait?

No. If the key is gone, forcing the door, lock, or ignition can cause damage that turns a key replacement into a bigger repair. It's better to leave the car alone, especially if the key snapped off or the lock is already worn. We can work from there without adding stress to the vehicle.

My key is gone, but I have a remote start or push-button system. Can you still help?

Often, yes. Those systems usually need the right replacement key or fob programmed to the car, not just a copied blade. The exact process depends on the model and year. If the car is equipped that way, tell us up front so we can come prepared for the job.
