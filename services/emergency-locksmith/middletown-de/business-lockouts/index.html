Business Lockouts in Middletown - common questions

My storefront in Middletown is locked up and the key won't turn. Do you always have to replace the lock?

No. A key that won't turn can come from a worn key, a dry or damaged cylinder, a misaligned latch, or a lock that's simply tired. We look at the hardware first and decide whether repair, rekeying, or replacement makes sense. If the body of the lock is still sound, replacing the whole unit may not be necessary.

What should we have ready when you come to open our office lockout?

Have someone who can show that the business belongs to you or that you're allowed to be there. If there are spare keys, bring them. It also helps to know whether the door has been acting up, whether the key was lost or broken, and whether the lock has been changed before. That keeps us focused on the right fix.

Can forcing the door make the lockout worse for a shop in Middletown?

Yes. Trying to pry or jam the door usually adds damage to the latch, strike, or frame, and that can turn a simple lockout into a hardware repair. If the door is already sticking, forcing it can bend parts that would otherwise still be usable. It's better to let us open it and then assess what needs attention.

Should we rekey or replace the lock after an employee key goes missing?

If the lock is otherwise in good shape, rekeying is often the practical choice because the missing key won't work anymore. If the hardware is old, inconsistent, or already giving trouble, replacement can be the better call. We look at the condition of the lock, the door, and how much wear the entry has already taken.

What if our business is in a new building and the original builder key is still out there somewhere?

That's a common concern in Middletown. In a newer building, the main issue may not be the lockout itself but the number of people who had access during construction. We can rekey the entry so the old keys no longer work and check whether the current hardware is worth keeping or should be replaced for better reliability.
