Commercial Lock Repair in North East - common questions

How do you decide whether a business door in North East needs repair or a full lock replacement?

The condition of the hardware decides it. If the lock is mostly intact and the issue is wear, loose parts, or alignment, repair is usually enough. If the case is cracked, the keyway is damaged, or the parts keep failing, replacement is the better long-term fix. The door's use matters too.

What should I have ready before you work on a commercial lock at my shop or office?

It helps to know which door is acting up, whether the key still turns, and whether the problem started after a key change, a slammed door, or new weather changes. If you have all the existing keys, bring them. If the door serves customers, let staff know the entrance may need brief handling while it's checked.

Can a worn lock create a bigger problem for a storefront near North East Main Street?

Yes. When a lock or latch drags, people often force the door, and that can bend the strike, wear out the closer, or damage the frame. A small alignment issue can turn into a door that won't latch at all. That's why commercial hardware should be checked before the damage spreads.

If I just want the key changed for an employee departure, do I need repair too?

Not always. Rekeying changes who can use the lock without replacing the whole unit. But if the lock is already sticking, grinding, or not catching cleanly, rekeying alone won't solve that. In those cases, it makes sense to repair the hardware at the same time so the door works properly.

What if the lock is on an older door in a building that gets a lot of summer traffic from Elk Neck visitors?

Older doors often need more than a simple key change because the wear is usually in the latch, strike, or closer as much as the lock. If the door gets heavy use, repair may still work, but replacement can be the better choice when the hardware can't hold alignment under daily traffic. The door has to close and latch the same way every time.
