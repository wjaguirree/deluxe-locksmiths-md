Before we come out, it helps to gather a few basics. Note which door is acting up, whether it's an exterior entry, a side door, or a door between work areas, and whether the trouble is dragging, slamming, or stopping short. If you can, take a clear photo of the door, the closer, and the hinge side from inside and outside. Door Closer Installation in Hockessin - common questions

How do you figure out whether my Hockessin door needs a new closer or just an adjustment?

We look at how the door moves from open to shut, then check the hinges, latch, arm, and closer body. If the unit still has solid spring power and the trouble is timing or latch speed, adjustment may solve it. If the closer leaks, binds, or can't control the door anymore, replacement is usually the better fix.

What should I have ready before you install a closer at my office or shop?

It helps to know which door is giving trouble, how it's used during the day, and whether you want the door to close fully every time or stay open under certain conditions. If the building has existing hardware that feels loose or damaged, telling us about that ahead of time helps us plan the job correctly.

Can a bad door closer damage the door or frame?

Yes. A door that slams can wear out hinges, loosen fasteners, and beat up the latch side over time. A door that doesn't latch can leave the opening unsecure and may also stress the hardware from repeated force. Getting the closer set correctly helps protect the door assembly.

Should I replace the closer or try to keep adjusting the one I have?

If the door is mostly sound and the closer still has working parts, adjustment may be enough. If the closer leaks oil, won't hold its settings, or is the wrong size for the door, replacement makes more sense. We match the hardware to the door instead of forcing an undersized closer to do the job.

What if my door is heavy or has special hardware on it?

That changes the choice of closer. Heavy doors, push bars, security hardware, or uneven frames can all affect how the closer performs. In those cases, we need to size and install the hardware around the real door conditions, not just the label on the box.
