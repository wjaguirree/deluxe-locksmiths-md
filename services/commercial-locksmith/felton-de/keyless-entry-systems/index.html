Keyless Entry Systems in Felton - common questions

How does a keypad entry system actually work for a small business in Felton?

A keypad system uses a code instead of a key. You enter the code, the lock releases, and the door opens. For a small office or shop, that means one person can manage access without making and collecting keys. Codes can usually be changed when staffing changes, which is useful for businesses with turnover or seasonal help.

What should I have ready before you set up keyless entry on my building?

It helps to know which door needs access, who should be able to use it, and whether you want a keypad, fob, or a fuller access-control setup. Photos of the door and lock area are useful too. If the building has an older lock or odd-sized door hardware, that affects what can be installed cleanly.

What's the biggest problem with putting keyless entry on an older commercial door?

The usual issue is fit. Older doors may not have the right prep for modern hardware, or the lock body and frame may not line up well enough for a clean install. Sometimes the door can be adapted. Other times a different lock style makes more sense. Power and wiring can also be a factor if the system needs them.

Should my shop use codes, fobs, or a mix of both?

Codes work well when a small group needs simple access and you want to update entry rights without issuing hardware. Fobs are useful when several people need to come and go often, especially if they're carrying tools or boxes. A mix can make sense for a business with a front door, an office, and a separate storage area.

What happens if the keypad or fob system stops working after hours at my place near Killens Pond State Park?

First, check whether the issue is the power supply, a dead fob battery, or a code problem. Some systems have a mechanical backup or a separate way in for the owner. If the hardware itself failed, the fix depends on the brand and how it was installed. A good setup should always leave you a way to secure the door while it's being serviced.
