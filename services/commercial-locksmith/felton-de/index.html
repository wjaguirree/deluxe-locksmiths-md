Commercial locksmith in Felton - common questions

How do you handle a storefront lock that keeps sticking in Felton?

We start by checking the lock, the latch, and the door alignment together. A sticking storefront lock is often more than one bad part. If the hardware is worn, we repair or replace the failed pieces and make sure the door closes cleanly, so the problem doesn't keep coming back after a few uses.

What should we have ready before you come to rekey our business door?

It helps to know which doors need to stay on the same key and which ones should be separate, especially if you have a front entrance, office, and storage room. If there are existing key marks, old master key notes, or a list of staff who still have keys, that also helps us plan the rekey cleanly.

Can a worn panic bar on our Felton shop door be repaired, or does it need to be replaced?

Sometimes it can be repaired if the problem is limited to a latch, spring, or fastener issue. If the bar is bent, the internal parts are failing, or the door opening no longer lines up, replacement may be the better fix. We look at the whole opening before deciding.

Should our small office use a master key system or keyless entry?

It depends on how many people need access and how much control you want over each door. A master key system works well when you want simple physical keys with different levels of access. Keyless entry is better when you want to change permissions without collecting keys. We can talk through both based on the way your office runs.

What if our commercial door was forced open near Killens Pond State Park and now the frame looks off?

If the frame, latch area, or closer mounting points were damaged, the lock alone may not solve it. We can repair the hardware, check the door swing, and see whether the strike or frame needs adjustment. If the opening is still out of line, we'll explain what can be corrected and what needs more work.
