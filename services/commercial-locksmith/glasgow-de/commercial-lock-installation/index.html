Commercial Lock Installation in Glasgow - common questions

What do you need from us before installing a commercial lock on our Glasgow storefront?

We need the door location, the type of opening, and what the door is used for. A front customer entrance, an employee-only door, and a storage room all call for different hardware. If you already know the current lock brand or have the old key, that helps, but it isn't required.

Will a new lock work with our existing keys and door hardware?

Sometimes, yes. If the cylinder style and hardware are compatible, we can often set things up to work with your current keying plan. If the lock body, strike, or door prep doesn't match, we may need to change more than one part to get a proper fit.

Can a new commercial lock solve a door that doesn't latch right near Route 896 traffic?

Sometimes the lock is only part of the problem. Heavy use, door movement, or a shifted frame can keep a door from latching cleanly. We check the whole opening and make sure the strike and hardware line up before we leave, because a good lock won't help if the door isn't set right.

Should we choose keyed entry, a deadbolt, or a mortise lock for our building?

It depends on the door and how much control you want. A keyed cylindrical lock works well on many offices and back doors. A mortise lock or other commercial setup may be better for a main entrance or a heavier door. We look at the opening and help choose hardware that fits the job.

Can you install commercial locks on a door at a plaza with limited loading access?

Yes. We do a lot of work where the door is in a busy lot or near a shared entrance, so we plan the install around the site. If the door is hard to reach, has limited parking, or sits behind a service area, we can still complete the work as long as the opening is accessible.
