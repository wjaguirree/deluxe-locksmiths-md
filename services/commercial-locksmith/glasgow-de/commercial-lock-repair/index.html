Signs It Needs Work

When a commercial lock starts going bad, the signs usually show up before it stops working completely. You may notice the key turning harder than usual, the latch catching on the strike, a loose handle, or a door that needs a little shoulder pressure to close. In places with heavy traffic, like shops near People's Plaza, that kind of wear builds fast because the door is getting used all day by customers, delivery drivers, and staff. If the key only works when you jiggle it, or the lock feels gritty instead of smooth, the parts inside are telling you they're worn out or out of alignment.

It's easier to deal with those problems early. Commercial Lock Repair in Glasgow - common questions

Why does the latch on my storefront door at People's Plaza keep missing the strike?

That usually means the door, frame, or hardware has shifted from repeated use. We check the latch, strike, hinges, and closer together so we're not fixing only one part of a bigger alignment problem. If the door has been dragging or closing hard, that's often part of the cause.

What should my manager gather before you come out for a broken office lock in Glasgow?

Have every key that still works, any access card or code details, and any old parts if someone already tried a repair. It also helps to know when the problem started and whether the door has been sticking, sagging, or slamming. That gives us a clearer starting point.

Is it a bad idea to keep using a door if the lock still sort of works?

Yes, if it's getting worse. A lock that only works part of the time can jam, leave the door unsecured, or damage the latch and strike. If the door is still usable, use it as little as possible until we can inspect it. Forcing it usually turns a repair into a bigger job.

Should we repair the lock, replace the cylinder, or change the whole hardware set?

It depends on what failed. If the body of the lock is sound, a repair or cylinder change may be enough. If the door has heavy wear, repeated misalignment, or damaged panic hardware, replacing more than one piece can make more sense. We'll lay out the options based on what the door actually needs.

Can you repair a back-door lock if the frame looks bent after a break-in near Glasgow Park?

Sometimes, but not always. If the frame is bent or the strike area is torn up, the lock may not hold correctly until the metal is repaired or rebuilt. We can assess the hardware and tell you whether a lock repair is enough or whether the door and frame need work too.
