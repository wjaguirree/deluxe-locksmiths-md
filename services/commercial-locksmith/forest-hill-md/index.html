Commercial locksmith in Forest Hill - common questions

How does a master key system usually work for a small business in Forest Hill?

A master key system lets different people open only the doors they need, while management keeps one key that works across the whole property. It's useful for offices, shops, and contractor spaces with stock rooms, side doors, or shared areas. The setup depends on the locks already on the doors and how you want access divided.

What should I have ready before a rekey or lock repair at my storefront?

It helps to know which doors are involved, who should keep access, and whether any old keys are still out there. If the door has been sticking, slamming, or not latching, point that out too. For rekeying, bring whatever current keys you still have so the locksmith can match the work to the full set.

I had an employee leave and I'm worried they still have a key. Is rekeying enough?

In many cases, yes. Rekeying changes the lock so the old key no longer works, which is often the right move after a staff change. If the hardware is worn, if a key broke off, or if multiple doors use different lock brands, you may need more than a simple rekey to get the building back under control.

Should I choose a panic bar or a regular commercial lock for an exit door?

For an exit door, a panic bar is usually the better fit when people need to leave quickly and the door still has to meet code and function smoothly. A regular lock may work on some doors, but it depends on the door's use and hardware. The safest choice is the one that matches the door's job, not just the frame it's in.

Can keyless entry work on a small office or shop that already has older hardware?

Sometimes it can, but not every old door is a clean fit. The condition of the door, the existing lockset, and how many people need access all matter. In some cases, the best move is to repair or replace the lock first, then add keyless entry where it makes sense.
