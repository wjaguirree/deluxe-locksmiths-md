Commercial locksmith in Joppatowne - common questions

My storefront door in Joppatowne still locks, but the key is getting harder to turn. Do I need repair, rekeying, or a new lock?

If the key is hard to turn but the lock still functions, the issue may be wear, dirt, corrosion, or door misalignment. Repair is usually the first thing to look at when the hardware is still serviceable. Rekeying only changes who can open it; it won't fix a bad cylinder. Replacement makes sense when the parts are worn out or rusted.

What should I tell you on the phone about a marina office or boat service shop lock on Route 40?

Tell us which door it is, what kind of hardware it has, and what it is doing wrong. A sticking key, broken latch, loose panic bar, or door that won't close all point to different fixes. If the lock is near the water, mention that too, because corrosion changes what can be repaired and what should be replaced.

If an employee leaves with a key, is rekeying enough for a business in Joppatowne?

Often, yes. Rekeying is the right move when the lock hardware is still in good shape and you just need old keys to stop working. If you also want a master key setup or the cylinder is already worn, it may be smarter to change more than just the pins. The door and the hardware decide that.

Can a panic bar on a commercial door be repaired, or does it usually need to be replaced?

It depends on what failed. A panic bar with a loose strike, worn latch, or minor alignment problem can often be repaired. If the bar is bent, badly corroded, or no longer meets the door the way it should, replacement is the better option. The door still has to open cleanly and close securely after the work.

What if my business uses one master key for several doors and one of the locks is acting up?

That usually calls for checking the whole system, not just one cylinder. Sometimes the bad lock can be rekeyed to stay in the master plan. Other times the worn hardware will fight the rest of the system and should be replaced. If the master setup is out of order, fixing one door the wrong way can create more trouble later.
