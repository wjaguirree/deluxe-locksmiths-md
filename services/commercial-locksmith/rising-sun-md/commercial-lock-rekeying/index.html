Commercial Lock Rekeying in Rising Sun - common questions

How does commercial rekeying work for a shop or office in Rising Sun?

The lock is opened, the cylinder is changed to a new key pattern, and the old keys stop working. The hardware stays in place. That makes it a good option when you want to control access again without changing every lock on the building.

What should I have ready before you rekey our building?

It helps to know which doors need to be included, whether you want one key to work multiple locks, and which keys are still in circulation. If you have a master key setup, bring that information too. A quick walk-through of the doors usually clears up the rest.

What's the risk if we keep using the same keys after an employee leaves?

Any copy already out there can still open the lock. That can put stock, records, tools, and locked storage at risk, especially if the building has side doors or separate rooms. Rekeying closes that gap by making the old key no longer work.

Should I rekey the locks or replace them on our commercial property?

If the hardware is in good shape, rekeying is usually the right first step. If a lock is damaged, worn out, or not suited to the door, replacement may make more sense. A locksmith can look at each door and tell you which parts need attention.

Can you rekey older storefront locks and newer door hardware on the same job?

Yes, as long as the locks are serviceable. Older hardware may need more care, while newer locks often take the rekey cleanly. The important part is matching the access plan to the building, so the same key works where you want it to and nowhere else.
