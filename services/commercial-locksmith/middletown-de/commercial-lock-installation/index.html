Commercial Lock Installation in Middletown - common questions

What do you need from me before a commercial lock installation in Middletown?

It helps to know what kind of door you have, whether it's a storefront, office, or rear entry, and what you want the lock to do. If you already have keys, bring them along. A photo of the door and current hardware can also help us plan the install and make sure the parts fit the opening.

Can you install new locks on a brand-new commercial space in Middletown?

Yes. New spaces often come with builder-grade hardware that works for the construction crew but not for daily business use. We can replace that with commercial-grade hardware that fits the door and the way your team will use it. If the frame or door hasn't been finished well, we can address that before the new lock goes on.

What if my storefront door already sticks or doesn't shut cleanly?

That matters a lot. A lock can't do its job well if the door and frame are out of line. We check how the door closes, where the latch meets the strike, and whether the hardware needs adjustment before or during the install. If the problem is in the door itself, we'll tell you what has to be corrected first.

Should I choose one key for all my business doors or different keys for each one?

That depends on how your business runs. One key can make day-to-day access easier for owners and managers. Separate keys can give you tighter control over a storage room, office, or back door. We can talk through the layout of your Middletown space and help you choose the setup that fits your team.

Can you install a new lock on a back door used for deliveries and staff entry?

Yes, and those doors often need careful attention because they get used differently from the front entrance. We look at the amount of traffic, the type of door, and how the latch should sit when the door is closed. Back-of-house entries deserve the same level of security as the public-facing door, sometimes more.
