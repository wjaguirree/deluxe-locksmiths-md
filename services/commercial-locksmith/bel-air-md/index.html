Commercial locksmith in Bel Air - common questions

If my office door in Bel Air is hard to lock, should I keep using it or shut it down?

If the door still closes and latches safely, you can usually keep using it until it's checked. If it won't latch, drags badly, or sticks open, stop using that entry for normal traffic and use another secure door if you have one. Forcing a failing commercial lock can bend the hardware or damage the frame.

What should I have ready before a locksmith comes to my storefront or office?

Have the affected keys, any spare keys, access cards, lock numbers if they're marked on the hardware, and the name of the door or suite that's having trouble. It also helps to know whether you want rekeying, repair, or a system change. Clear the area around the door so the hardware is easy to inspect.

I'm worried a former employee still has access to my building. What's the usual fix?

For a lot of Bel Air businesses, the first step is rekeying the affected locks so old keys stop working. If the building uses a master key system, that may need to be updated too. If the hardware is already worn or the door has multiple access points, replacing selected locks or switching to keypad access may make more sense.

Should I choose a master key system or keyless entry for a small business?

A master key system works well when you want different keys for staff, managers, and specific rooms, while still keeping one controlled structure. Keyless entry is better when you want to change access without collecting keys. The right choice depends on how many people need access, how often that access changes, and which doors need tighter control.

What if the panic bar or storefront lock is damaged but the door still opens?

Treat that as a real security and safety issue, even if the door still functions. Don't prop it open, bypass the hardware, or tape it in place. Keep the door in the safest workable state and have the bar, latch, or storefront lock checked before normal business hours or foot traffic make the damage worse.
