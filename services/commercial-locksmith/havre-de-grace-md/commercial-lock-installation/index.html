Commercial Lock Installation in Havre de Grace - common questions

How is a commercial lock installation on a storefront door in Havre de Grace usually handled?

First the door, frame, and existing prep get checked to see what fits and what needs adjustment. Then the right commercial-grade lock is installed and aligned so the latch meets the strike cleanly. On older downtown doors, the job often includes correcting wear or patching past mismatched hardware so the new lock works without rubbing or binding.

What should I have ready before you install locks on my office or shop door?

It helps to know which door needs the new lock, who needs access, and whether the door already has other hardware like a closer or panic bar. If you have the current keying or any brand information from the old lock, that can also help. The more the existing setup is understood, the cleaner the install can be.

What usually goes wrong when a commercial lock is put on the wrong way?

The biggest problems are misaligned strikes, loose latch engagement, and using parts that don't match the door type. That can leave the door hard to close, hard to lock, or easy to wear out. In a busy place, that turns into daily frustration and can weaken the door and frame over time.

Should I choose a deadbolt, a mortise lock, or a lever set for a business door?

It depends on the door, the frame, and how the space is used. A storefront entry, a side office, and a back service door often need different hardware. The right choice is the one that fits the existing prep, meets your access needs, and holds up to the way people actually use that door.

Can you install new commercial locks on an older door near the water without replacing the whole door?

Often, yes. If the door and frame are still sound, new commercial hardware can usually be fitted with the right adjustments. The main question is whether the wood or metal has enough solid material left for the lock, strike, and fasteners to hold properly. If not, repairs may be needed first.
