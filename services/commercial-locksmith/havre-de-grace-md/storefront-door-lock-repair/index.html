Storefront Door Lock Repair in Havre de Grace - common questions

Why won't my storefront door lock catch even when the key turns in Havre de Grace?

That usually means the door is out of alignment or the latch parts are worn. On an aluminum storefront door, the pivot, threshold, or strike can shift enough that the lock no longer lines up. We check the whole opening, not just the cylinder, so we can fix the part that is actually causing the failure.

What should we have ready before you come look at our storefront door?

Have the door unlocked if you can, and tell us exactly what it is doing: sticking, sagging, spinning key, loose handle, or not catching. If the door has been repaired before, that helps too. We'll work with the hardware on site and figure out whether the issue is the lock, the pivot, or the frame side.

Is it safe to keep using a shop door in Havre de Grace if the lock still sort of works?

It's risky. A lock that only partly catches can give a false sense of security and may fail under normal use. It can also let the door rack more each day, which puts more strain on the closer and hinges. We recommend getting it checked before the wear turns into a bigger hardware problem.

Should we repair the mortise cylinder or replace the whole storefront lock?

That depends on what is worn. If the cylinder is the only damaged part, repair or replacement of that piece may be enough. If the hook bolt, latch, or body of the lock is failing, a broader repair makes more sense. We look at the whole door so you're not paying to fix the wrong part.

Can you fix a storefront door that has been forced or bent at the bottom?

Sometimes, yes. If the threshold, pivot, or lock side shifted but the door and frame are still sound, we can often realign the hardware and restore the close. If the glass, frame, or core hardware is badly damaged, we'll tell you what can still be saved and what needs replacement.
