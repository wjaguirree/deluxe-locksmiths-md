Panic Bar Installation in Delaware City - common questions

How does panic bar installation work for a shop in Delaware City?

We start by looking at the door, frame, and how the space is used. Then we choose the right exit device, fit it to the opening, and adjust it so the door latches and releases properly. If the door needs prep work or replacement parts, we handle that as part of the installation.

What should I have ready before you install a panic bar on my Delaware City building?

It helps to know which door needs the device, who uses that door, and whether it has had trouble closing or latching. If you have building rules, fire code notes, or landlord requirements, bring those along. That gives us a clear picture of what the opening needs before we start.

Can a panic bar fix a door that already sticks or does not latch right?

Sometimes the hardware is only part of the problem. If the door is warped, the frame is out of line, or the closer is weak, a new panic bar alone won't solve it. We can tell you what needs to be corrected so the exit device works the way it should.

Should I use a rim device or a vertical rod device on my Delaware City exit door?

That depends on the door, the opening, and how much traffic it sees. A simple single door often works well with a rim device, while some wider or heavier openings need different hardware. We look at the setup and recommend the style that fits the door instead of guessing.

What if my tenant space in Delaware City already has an old exit bar that is worn out?

We can replace worn hardware, but we still need to make sure the door and frame are in good shape. If the old bar was installed the wrong way or is missing parts, we may need to correct the opening before a new device goes on. That keeps the door dependable and easier to use.
