Commercial Lock Installation in Abingdon - common questions

What usually gets installed on a commercial door in Abingdon?

It depends on the door and how the business uses it. Common choices include commercial levers, deadbolts, mortise hardware, and storefront cylinders. For offices and back entrances, the goal is to match the lock to the door type, the amount of traffic, and whether staff need keyed access or a more secure exit setup.

What should I have ready before the locksmith comes to install a new business lock?

Have the door accessible, know which entry is being worked on, and be ready to point out any issues you've noticed, like sticking, loose hardware, or a latch that doesn't catch. If it's a tenant space, it also helps to know who has authority to approve the work and whether existing keys need to keep working.

Can a bad install create problems later even if the lock itself is good?

Yes. If the strike, latch, and door alignment aren't set correctly, a good lock can still bind, not close cleanly, or wear out faster than it should. On commercial doors, small fit issues matter because the hardware gets used a lot. A proper install is as much about alignment as it is about the lock body itself.

How do I decide between a standard keyed lock and heavier commercial hardware?

Think about how often the door is used and what the door protects. A back office or storage room may only need basic keyed access, while a storefront or main employee entrance usually needs heavier-duty hardware that can handle more traffic. The door material, frame condition, and fire or exit requirements can also affect the choice.

What if my building in Abingdon has an older door or mixed hardware already on it?

That's common. Older doors may need a lock that fits an existing cutout, or the frame may need adjustment before new hardware can work correctly. Sometimes the right answer is replacing the lock; other times it's reworking the strike or using commercial hardware that better matches the door's condition and the way the building is used.
