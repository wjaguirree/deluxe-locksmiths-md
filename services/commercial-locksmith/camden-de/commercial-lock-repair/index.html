Commercial Lock Repair in Camden - common questions

Why does the lock on my Camden storefront keep sticking even after we jiggle the key?

That usually means the lock is only part of the problem. On commercial doors, the strike, hinges, closer, and door alignment all affect how the lock works. If the door is out of line, a fresh cylinder may still bind. A proper repair checks the whole door so the fix holds up.

What should I have ready before you come repair the lock at my business in Camden?

Have the door unlocked if possible, and know which entrance is causing trouble. If there's a master key system, bring any keys that are acting up and let us know who should still have access. It also helps to point out whether the issue is with the key, the latch, the deadbolt, or the door closing.

Could a bad commercial lock repair leave my door less secure?

Yes. If someone only changes a part without checking fit, the door may latch loosely or not throw fully, which creates a security problem. A proper repair should leave the door closing cleanly, the lock engaging all the way, and the hardware working together the way it should.

Should I repair the existing lock or replace it on a busy door in Camden?

If the cylinder is worn but the body and door are still solid, repair can make sense. If the lock is damaged, mismatched, or the hardware has been patched too many times, replacement is usually the better call. The right choice depends on the condition of the door, the frame, and the amount of use it gets.

Can you fix a lock on a rear delivery door that's getting hard to close?

Yes, and that's worth checking early. Rear and side doors often take a beating from carts, deliveries, and repeated use. If the door won't close right, the lock may be fine but unable to line up. We can inspect the hardware on site and repair what's actually causing the failure.
