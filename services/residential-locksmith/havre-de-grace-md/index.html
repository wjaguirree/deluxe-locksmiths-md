Home lock work in Havre de Grace usually comes down to three choices: repair, rekey, or replace. Residential locksmith in Havre de Grace - common questions

Should I rekey or replace the locks after buying a house in Havre de Grace?

If the locks are in decent shape and you just want the old keys to stop working, rekeying is usually the better first step. If the hardware is worn, loose, or badly matched to the door, replacement makes more sense. We look at the condition of the lock and the door before we recommend one path.

What should I have ready when you come to work on my home locks?

Please have the door unlocked if possible, and make sure someone who can approve the work is home. If you know which doors are giving trouble, point those out first. It also helps to mention any recent changes, like a move, a lost key, new tenants, or a door that has started sticking.

My front door in Downtown Havre de Grace is hard to lock. Does that mean I need a new lock?

Not always. Sometimes the issue is alignment, worn parts, or a latch that isn't seating right. In those cases, repair can be enough. If the key still feels rough after the adjustment, or the mechanism is worn out, replacement may be the better fix.

Can you keep my existing keys if I want better security at my house in Bulle Rock?

Usually not with a full security upgrade, since high-security locks and smart locks often use different keying or different hardware altogether. If you want to keep the same general setup, rekeying can help without changing every part. We can walk you through what stays the same and what changes.

What if my mailbox lock is jammed but the house locks are fine?

That's common. Mailbox locks are separate from the door hardware, so we can replace or repair the mailbox lock without touching the rest of the house. If the key broke off, the lock is corroded, or the cylinder won't turn, replacement is often the cleanest solution.
