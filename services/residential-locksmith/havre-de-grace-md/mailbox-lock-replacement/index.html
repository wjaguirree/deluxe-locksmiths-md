Mailbox Lock Replacement in Havre de Grace - common questions

How does mailbox lock replacement work on a house in Havre de Grace?

We remove the old lock, match the new cylinder to the mailbox style, and test the door so it opens and latches cleanly. If the box was damaged by a bent cam, worn latch, or past repair, we correct that as part of the job so the new lock doesn't inherit the same problem.

What should I have ready before you replace my mailbox lock?

Have the mailbox accessible and know whether it's a home box or part of a shared cluster unit. If you still have the old key, bring it. If the lock was forced or the door is bent, let us know. That helps us bring the right replacement parts and avoid a second trip.

What's the biggest mistake people make with a mailbox lock in Havre de Grace?

The biggest mistake is forcing the wrong lock to fit. That can crack the door, ruin the cam, or leave the box closing loosely. We also see repairs where the lock works for a day but binds later because the alignment was ignored. A proper result should feel smooth and secure, not patched together.

Should I replace the whole mailbox or just the lock?

If the box is solid and only the lock is worn, replacing the lock is usually the better move. If the door is rusted, bent, or missing the latch point, a new lock alone may not solve it. We look at the condition of the box and tell you which parts actually need work.

Can you handle a cluster mailbox unit if my apartment compartment key stopped working?

Yes, we handle cluster-box units and other shared mailbox setups. Those jobs need the correct lock type and careful access because one compartment can be affected without touching the rest. If the key failed because the lock is worn or damaged, we replace the lock and verify that the compartment opens and closes as it should.
