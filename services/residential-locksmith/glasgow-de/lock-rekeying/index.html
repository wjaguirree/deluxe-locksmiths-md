Lock Rekeying in Glasgow - common questions

How does rekeying a house lock work after I move into a home in Glasgow?

We keep the lock hardware on your doors and change the pins inside the cylinder so the old keys no longer work. Then we cut or verify new keys for the new setup. It's a good first step after a move because it gives you control without replacing every lock on the house.

What should I have ready when you come to rekey my locks?

Have the doors accessible and know which locks you want included. If you still have any old keys, bring them along. For a home with multiple doors, it also helps to tell us which key you want to use everywhere and whether any locks were changed before you moved in.

Is there a risk that my older lock in Glasgow can't be rekeyed?

Yes. Some older locks are too worn, corroded, or altered from past repairs to rekey cleanly. We can usually tell after opening the cylinder. If the parts are too damaged to trust, we'll say so and explain the options instead of forcing a lock that won't hold up.

Should I rekey the locks or replace them on a new place in Glasgow?

If the hardware is solid, rekeying is usually the right first move. If the lock is loose, mismatched, or failing to turn smoothly, replacement may make more sense. We look at the condition of the door and lock, then help you choose the option that fits the hardware you already have.

Can you rekey all the locks on a house if one door has a different brand or a second key?

Often, yes. Mixed hardware is common in homes here, especially when doors were added or updated at different times. We can usually rekey matching cylinders to one key and tell you plainly if one lock is too different, too worn, or built in a way that needs another approach.
