Door Lock Repair in Glasgow - common questions

Why does my front door in Glasgow close, but the latch won't catch right?

That usually means the lock and the door are out of alignment. The strike plate may have shifted, the door may be sagging, or the latch may be worn. We check the hardware and the fit together, then repair what's causing the latch to miss instead of forcing the door to keep working that way.

What should I have ready before you repair the door lock?

It helps to know which door is acting up and what it's been doing: sticking, not closing, or needing to be lifted to latch. If there are other issues, like a loose knob or a damaged frame, point those out too. That gives us a clear picture before we start the repair.

Is it a security problem if I can still lock the door, but it feels rough?

Yes, it can be. A rough lock often means the parts are rubbing, shifting, or starting to fail. The door may not be fully engaging every time, which leaves a gap in security. It can also wear the hardware down faster until it stops working when you need it to.

Do you repair the lock itself, or do you replace the whole door hardware?

We do whichever makes sense for the condition of the door. If the issue is alignment or a part that can be repaired, we'll repair it. If the lock body or strike is worn beyond that, replacement may be the better option. We look at the setup first and go from there.

Can you fix a lock that only gives trouble when the door swells or shifts in certain weather?

Often, yes. That usually points to a fit issue, not just a bad lock. We can adjust the strike, check the latch engagement, and repair the hardware so it works more consistently. If the door itself is moving too much, we'll let you know what's causing that part of the problem.
