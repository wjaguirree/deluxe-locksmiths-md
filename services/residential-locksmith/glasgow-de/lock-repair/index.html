Lock Repair in Glasgow - common questions

How do I know if my Glasgow front door lock needs repair or just adjustment?

If the key turns normally when the door is open but binds when the door is shut, the lock may be fine and the alignment is off. If it feels rough either way, the lock mechanism itself may need repair. We look at the latch, strike, screws, and cylinder together so the real problem gets fixed.

What should I have ready before you come out to fix a house lock in Glasgow?

Please make sure the door is accessible and let us know which lock is acting up, such as the front entry, back door, or garage entry. If you have a spare key or know whether the lock has been sticking, that helps us narrow it down faster. We can work from there without guesswork.

Can a sticking lock in my Glasgow home damage the door if I keep using it?

It can. Forcing a latch or deadbolt can wear the strike, loosen screws, and make the door harder to close properly. In some cases it can also damage the key or cylinder. If the lock is dragging, it's better to have it checked before the problem spreads to the frame or hardware.

Should I repair the lock or replace it if the deadbolt feels loose?

If the body of the lock is loose but the core parts are still sound, repair is often the better move. If the internal parts are worn, stripped, or damaged, replacement may make more sense. We'll inspect the lock and tell you which direction fits the hardware instead of replacing it just because it's easier.

What if my lock works, but only after I push or lift the door a little?

That usually points to alignment, not a bad key. The door may have settled, the strike may be off, or the latch may not be meeting the opening cleanly. We can adjust the hardware so the lock engages without extra pressure. If needed, we'll also check for worn parts that are making the problem worse.
