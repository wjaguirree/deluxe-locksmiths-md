The difference between a temporary fix and a proper repair matters. A temporary fix can keep a sticky lock usable for a while, but it may still bind, catch, or fail when the weather changes or the door settles. A proper repair looks at the whole setup: the lock body, latch, strike, door alignment, and the way the hardware is mounted. If a part is worn out, we replace it. If the lock is salvageable, we clean, adjust, and correct the issue so it works the way it should instead of being coaxed along.

That matters in rentals and student housing, where turnover is constant and small hardware problems become repeated calls if they are left half-solved. We work on site, at the home or property, so the fix is matched to the door in front of us. If a rekey is enough, we do that. If the lock is past repair, we'll say so and install hardware that fits the door and the way the property is used.

Residential locksmith in Newark - common questions

If I just moved into a Newark rental, do I need a rekey or a full lock change?

A rekey is usually the first thing to look at if the lock hardware is in good shape. It lets us change which keys work without replacing the whole lock. If the deadbolt is worn, damaged, or low quality, replacement can be the better call. We can inspect the door and tell you which path makes sense.

What should I have ready before you come out to my house near Main Street?

Have the door accessible, the current key if you still have one, and a clear idea of what happened before the problem started. If it's a rental, it also helps to know whether the landlord wants rekeying or replacement. That gives us a cleaner start and helps us match the hardware to the door.

My front lock is sticking, but it still works. Can it be repaired, or does it need to be replaced?

Sometimes it can be repaired if the issue is wear, dirt, or a strike plate that is out of line. If the cylinder is worn out, the key is badly degraded, or the lock has been forced, replacement is often the better long-term fix. We look at the whole door setup before recommending either one.

For a student rental, is a smart lock a better choice than changing the deadbolt every term?

It depends on the property and how it's managed. Smart locks can make turnover simpler when codes need to change often, but they have to be installed correctly and chosen for the right door. A standard deadbolt may still be the better option if the setup is basic or the property owner wants something straightforward.

My mailbox key is missing and the box is old. Is that something you can still handle?

In many cases, yes. Mailbox locks can often be replaced even when the box itself is older, as long as the hardware is accessible and in usable condition. If the box is damaged or the lock is a nonstandard setup, we'll explain what can be done and whether replacement parts are needed.
