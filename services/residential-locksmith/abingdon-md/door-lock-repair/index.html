Door Lock Repair in Abingdon - common questions

Why does my door lock turn but the door still won't latch in my Abingdon townhouse?

That usually means the lock isn't the only problem. The door or frame may have shifted, the hinges may be loose, or the strike plate may be out of line. In townhouse construction, even a small change in alignment can keep the latch from catching cleanly.

What should I have ready before someone comes to repair a residential door lock?

It helps to know which door is acting up, whether the key still works, and whether the issue is with locking, latching, or both. If you've had any recent work on the door, frame, or hinges, mention that too. That gives a clearer starting point for the repair.

Is a sticky lock in a rental in Constant Friendship a security risk or just wear and tear?

It can be either. Sometimes it's just worn hardware or a door that's shifted. Other times the latch barely catches, which means the door is not securing the way it should. If the door has to be forced shut, it's worth getting checked before the problem gets worse.

Should I repair the existing lock or replace it on my Abingdon front door?

If the body of the lock is solid and the problem is alignment, a repair often makes sense. If the springs are worn, the keyway is damaged, or parts keep failing, replacement may be the better call. The door's condition and how much the hardware has been used matter more than the age alone.

Can an old deadbolt be repaired if the door was painted over or patched before?

Sometimes, yes. Paint buildup, patching, and old screws can all interfere with how the latch moves and seats. If the hardware itself is still usable, cleaning, adjusting, or resetting it may solve the problem. If the parts are corroded or stripped, repair may not hold.
