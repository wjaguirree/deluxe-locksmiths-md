For a house in the Felton town grid or a place off DE-12, that often matters more than swapping out the whole lock, because the weak point is usually not the metal on the door. Lock Rekeying in Felton - common questions

How does rekeying work on the front door of a house in Felton?

The lock comes apart, the old key pattern is removed, and new pins are set to match a different key. The hardware stays on the door. If the lock is in decent shape, this is a practical way to stop old keys from working without changing the knob or deadbolt.

What should I have ready before I ask for a rekey on my place near the Route 13 corridor?

It helps to know which doors you want changed, how many locks are involved, and whether any of them already use the same key. If you have all the current keys, bring them. If a lock has been loose, sticky, or damaged, mention that so the job can be planned around it.

Is it a problem if I leave the old keys out there after moving into a house in Felton?

Yes, that can be a real security gap. Previous owners, tenants, contractors, family members, or anyone who got a copy may still be able to open the door. Rekeying removes that old access without forcing you to replace every piece of hardware.

Should I rekey the locks or replace them on an older house in town?

If the lock works well and you just need new keys to fit, rekeying is usually the better choice. If the hardware is worn out, broken, or mismatched, replacement may make more sense. A worn key cylinder, a damaged latch, or missing parts can limit what rekeying can solve.

Can the same key be set up for all the doors on my house and outbuildings?

Often, yes, if the locks are the same type and in good condition. Many homeowners like one key for the front door, back door, and other accessible entry points. Some specialty locks or older hardware may not match up, especially on outbuildings or mixed brands.
