Residential locksmith in Rising Sun - common questions

If I just moved to a house in Rising Sun, should we rekey every lock or replace them?

If the locks are in decent shape, rekeying is usually the first thing we look at. It lets you keep the existing hardware and change which keys work. If a deadbolt is worn, doesn't latch well, or feels loose in the door, replacement may be the better call for that opening.

What should I have ready before you come out to my place in Rising Sun?

Have the doors you want worked on accessible, and gather any keys you still have for those locks. If it's a rental, tenant house, or shared property, let us know which doors need the same key and which should stay separate. That helps us set up the right keying plan.

Can a lock be repaired if the key still turns but the door won't latch right?

Yes, sometimes. The problem may be the lock itself, the strike, the door alignment, or worn parts inside the hardware. We check the whole setup. If the lock body is still solid, repair can be the right fix. If the parts are too worn, replacement is the better option.

How do we decide between rekeying and installing a high-security lock on a farm house or outbuilding?

Rekeying is best when the hardware already works well and you mainly need control over the keys. High-security hardware makes more sense when you want stronger protection, tighter key control, or better resistance to tampering. Outbuildings with equipment, tools, or feed often benefit from that upgrade.

Can you make one key work for the house, mailbox, and side building in Rising Sun?

Sometimes for the house and some outbuildings, yes, if the locks are compatible. Mailbox locks are different and often need their own key or a separate replacement. We'll look at each lock and tell you what can be matched together and what needs its own key setup.
