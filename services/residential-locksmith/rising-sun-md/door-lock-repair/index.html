Door lock repair in Rising Sun usually comes down to whether the lock body is still sound. That makes sense for farmhouse doors, tenant houses, and outbuildings where the lock has wear but the key system still works fine.

Rekeying is different. We use it when the lock works, but you don't want the same key in circulation after a move, a lost key, or a tenant change. Replacing is the right call when the cylinder is damaged, the parts are badly worn, or the hardware no longer fits the door well. On older homes and busy side doors off US-1, we look at the door, the frame, and the lock together so you're not just swapping parts without fixing the cause.

We don't push replacement when repair will do the job. If the lock can be adjusted, tightened, or rebuilt and still hold up, that's usually the better path. On newer homes and additions, the problem is often different: tighter hardware, newer deadbolts, and door slabs that can go out of square after a hinge loosens or a frame settles. Door Lock Repair in Rising Sun - common questions

My front door in Rising Sun sticks when I try to lock it. Do you repair the lock or the door frame?

We look at both. A sticking lock is often really a door alignment issue, a loose strike plate, or worn hardware that's no longer meeting cleanly. If the lock itself is still solid, we repair and adjust what's out of line so the latch catches without forcing the door.

What should I have ready when you come to fix a door lock on my house or outbuilding?

It helps to have the door unlocked if possible, all keys you use for that lock, and any old hardware you've kept. If it's a rental or an outbuilding, let us know which door has the problem and whether anything has changed recently, like a moved tenant or a lost key.

I'm worried the lock is bad because the key turns hard. Could forcing it make things worse?

Yes. Forcing a stiff lock can bend parts, snap a key, or damage the cylinder so a repair turns into a replacement. If the key is catching, stop using pressure on it. We can inspect the lock, clean up the issue if it's minor, or replace worn parts if the hardware has failed.

Should I rekey my lock or replace it after buying a house in Cecil County?

If the lock is in decent shape and you just want to control who has a key, rekeying is usually the better choice. If the hardware is loose, corroded, or the latch won't line up right, replacement makes more sense. We'll help you sort out which one fits the door instead of guessing.

Can you fix an old lock on a farm outbuilding if there isn't a matching key anymore?

Often, yes. If the lock is intact, we can usually repair it and rekey it to a new key. If the cylinder is badly worn or parts are missing, replacement may be the better route. The condition of the hardware matters more than the age of the building.
