Lock Rekeying in North East - common questions

How does a residential rekey work on the locks in my North East house?

The locksmith removes the cylinder from the knob or deadbolt, takes out the old pin set, and installs new pins that match a new key. The lock body stays on the door. After that, the old key won't work, and the new key becomes the only one that lines up the pins correctly.

What should I have ready before you rekey my doors?

Have the doors accessible and know which locks you want changed. If you have the current working key, bring it. If you don't, that can still be handled in some cases, but the lock may need to come apart differently. It also helps to know whether the same key should work on every door or just certain ones.

What if a lock in my house is already sticky or hard to turn?

That can mean worn pins, a tired cylinder, or damage from a bad key or past force. Rekeying may fix the keying problem if the parts are still sound. If the inside parts are bent, cracked, or badly worn, the lock may need replacement instead of a rekey.

Should I rekey the locks or replace them after buying a home in North East?

If the hardware is solid, rekeying is usually the cleaner option because it keeps the existing knobs and deadbolts in place. Replace the lock if it's loose, corroded, damaged, or an old model that no longer holds up well. For many homes, rekeying is enough to take control of the keys right away.

Can one key be made to open all the doors on my house, including the deadbolts?

Often, yes, if the lock brands and cylinder types will work together and the hardware is in decent shape. A locksmith can set up the pinning so one key works across multiple locks. If the locks are mixed brands or mismatched models, that may limit what can be combined.
