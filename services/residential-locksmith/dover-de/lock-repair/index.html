Lock Repair in Dover - common questions

My front door in Dover locks, but the key feels rough. Does that usually mean the whole lock has to be replaced?

Not always. Rough turning often comes from misalignment, worn screws, a dirty cylinder, or a door that has shifted a little. A repair can sometimes solve it without changing the whole lock. If internal parts are worn out or damaged, replacement may be the better call.

What should I have ready before you come out to fix a lock on my house in Dover?

It helps to know which door is acting up, whether it's a deadbolt, knob, or handle set, and whether the problem is new or has been building over time. If the key is sticking, bring the key you normally use. A quick description of what the lock is doing saves a lot of back-and-forth.

Can a loose lock on an older home near The Green be repaired if the door frame is the real problem?

Yes, in many cases. A lock can often be adjusted to work properly even when the frame or strike area has shifted some. If the wood is badly worn or split, the frame may need repair too. The important thing is figuring out whether the lock, the door, or the frame is causing the issue.

If my key works in one lock but not another, is repair enough or should I replace the hardware?

That depends on what's failing. If the problem is alignment, a sticky latch, or loose hardware, repair is often enough. If the cylinder is worn, the key is damaged, or parts inside the lock are breaking down, replacement may make more sense. A proper check can usually sort that out.

After a move near Dover Air Force Base, can you repair a lock that was installed wrong by the landlord or builder?

Yes, that's a common situation. Some locks are mounted crooked, set too tight, or paired with a strike that never lined up right. Those problems can often be corrected by adjusting the hardware and reworking the fit. If the lock itself is the wrong style for the door, a different setup may be needed.
