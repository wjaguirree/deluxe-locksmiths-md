Residential locksmith in Cecilton - common questions

If I just bought a house in Cecilton, should the locks be rekeyed or replaced?

Rekeying is often enough if the locks are in good shape and you want a new key set for the same hardware. Replacement makes more sense when the lock is worn, mismatched, rusted, or not giving you the security you want. We look at the door, the cylinder, and the deadbolt before recommending one way or the other.

What should I have ready when you come out to work on my front door?

Please make sure we can reach the door and test it fully. If you have the current working key, a spare, or the old hardware packaging, that can help. For a smart lock, have the battery access handy and know whether the keypad, app, or both are giving you trouble.

My deadbolt turns, but the door still feels loose. Is that a lock problem?

Sometimes it is, but not always. A loose-feeling door can come from a worn strike, a shifted door frame, or a deadbolt that isn't fully catching. We check the bolt, latch, and alignment together so the fix matches the actual problem instead of only changing one part.

Should I choose a smart lock or a high-security deadbolt for my older home?

It depends on how you use the door. A smart lock is handy when you want keypad entry or key control without extra keys floating around. A high-security deadbolt is better when you want stronger physical protection and a more traditional setup. We can match either option to the door without forcing a poor fit.

Can a mailbox lock be replaced if the key is lost and the box is old?

Usually, yes. The key is whether the mailbox body and lock opening are still in usable shape. If the lock is rusted in place or the mailbox has been bent, replacement may need a little more work. We can inspect the hardware and tell you whether the lock can be swapped cleanly.
