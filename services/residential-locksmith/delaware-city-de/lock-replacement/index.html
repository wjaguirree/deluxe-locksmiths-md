Lock Replacement in Delaware City - common questions

How do you replace a house lock in Delaware City without damaging the door?

We remove the old lock, check the bore and screw pattern, then fit new hardware that matches the door as closely as possible. If the door or strike is out of line, we adjust that too. The goal is a clean install that locks smoothly and doesn't chew up the wood or trim.

What should I have ready before you replace the locks on my Delaware City home?

Have the doors you want changed accessible and, if possible, the old keys for the existing locks. If there's a storm door, deadbolt, or a side entry with a different setup, point that out first. It also helps to know whether you want the same key used for more than one door.

My key still works, but the deadbolt feels loose. Does that mean I need a full replacement?

Not always. A loose deadbolt can come from mounting screws, a misaligned strike, or a worn cylinder. If the lock body is still sound, a repair may be enough. If the keyway is worn, the bolt binds, or the hardware is damaged, replacement is usually the better call.

Should I replace just one lock or all the exterior locks on my house?

If one lock failed because of age or damage, replacing that one may be enough. If the other locks are the same age, have different keys, or show the same wear, changing them together can be simpler. We can also set them up to use one key where the hardware allows it.

Can you replace a lock on an older Delaware City house if the door or frame is out of square?

Yes, but the fit has to be checked carefully. Older doors often settle, swell, or have patched holes from past hardware. We can replace the lock and make the alignment work with the door as it is. If the frame is badly off, we'll explain what the lock can and can't fix.
