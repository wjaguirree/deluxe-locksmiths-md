Lock Rekeying in Joppatowne - common questions

How does rekeying a house lock work in Joppatowne?

We take the lock apart enough to reset the internal pins so the old key no longer works. Then we cut or match a new key to the new pin pattern and test the lock at the door. It keeps the same hardware in place, which is useful when the lock still fits the door well.

What should I have ready before having my locks rekeyed after moving in?

Have the keys you still have, and know which doors you want matched together. It helps to point out any lock that sticks, binds, or looks corroded. If there are gates, side doors, or a back entry, make sure we can reach them without hunting around the property.

Can rekeying fix a lock that's been exposed to salt air near the water?

Sometimes it can, if the problem is mainly the keying and not broken internal parts. Salt air can seize components and leave a lock feeling rough or unreliable. If the cylinder or hardware is too worn, rekeying won't solve every issue, and replacement may be the better answer.

Should I rekey the lock or replace it after a tenant or roommate moves out?

If the lock body is solid and the door hardware still works properly, rekeying is usually the practical choice. If the lock is bent, loose, or has been forced, replacement may be smarter. The decision usually comes down to the condition of the hardware, not just the keys.

Can you rekey every lock on the same house to one key?

Often, yes, if the locks are the same type or compatible. That can include several exterior doors so one key works across the house. If one lock is a different brand or a special security design, it may not match the rest without changing parts.
