Deadbolt Installation in Chesapeake City - common questions

How is a new deadbolt installed on an older Chesapeake City front door?

First the door and frame get checked for fit, because a deadbolt can't work right if the latch line is off. Then the lock bore and strike are set to match the door, the hardware is mounted, and the bolt is tested for full throw. On older wood doors, the strike and screws matter as much as the lock body.

What should I have ready before you install a deadbolt at my house in Chesapeake City?

Clear the area around the door so the frame, hinges, and jamb are easy to inspect. If the door has been repaired before, keep any old lock parts handy. It also helps to know whether you want the deadbolt matched to existing keys or replaced as a separate lock.

My deadbolt works sometimes and then binds. Is that a lock problem or a door problem?

It can be either, but in many Chesapeake City homes it's a fit issue. If the door has settled or swollen, the bolt may be hitting the edge of the strike instead of sliding in cleanly. A new lock won't fix that by itself. The door, strike, and screws all need to be checked together.

Should I repair my old deadbolt or replace it with a new one?

If the body of the lock is sound and the problem is only alignment, a repair may be enough. If the bolt is worn, the key action is sloppy, or the strike area has been patched up more than once, replacement is usually the better choice. The frame has to support the lock either way.

Can a deadbolt be added to a door that never had one?

Usually, yes. The door has to have enough solid material for the lock and strike to be mounted correctly. On some thin or damaged doors, reinforcement is needed first. For older doors, especially in houses that have seen a lot of use, the install has to be planned so the bolt lands in strong wood, not weak trim.
