Door Lock Repair in Forest Hill - common questions

How do you tell if my door needs repair or just an adjustment in Forest Hill?

We look at how the door behaves with it open and then again when it's shut. If the key turns fine but the latch misses the strike, that points to alignment. If the key is rough even with the door open, the lock itself may be worn. The difference saves time and keeps us from fixing the wrong part.

What should I have ready before you work on my front door?

If it's a rental, have the landlord's okay first. For a house, it helps to know whether the problem started after moving in, after new paint, or after the door began sagging. If you still have the key that came with the lock, bring it. Small details like that can point us to the real cause.

Is it a bad sign if my lock works only when I lift the door a little?

That usually means the door or hinges have shifted and the latch isn't lining up cleanly. It's a common issue on doors that settle over time, especially in newer construction. The lock may still be fine, but forcing the door to make it work can wear out the latch and strike faster.

Should I repair the lock or replace it if the key is getting harder to turn?

If the cylinder is still sound and the trouble is from dirt, wear, or poor alignment, repair may be enough. If the key is badly worn, the hardware is damaged, or the inside parts are failing, replacement can make more sense. We check the whole setup before recommending either path.

My builder's lock in Forest Hill was given to several trades. Can you still repair it?

Usually, yes. If the hardware is still in good shape, we can repair the lock and then rekey it so the old key no longer works. If the lock was forced, overused, or installed badly, we'll say so plainly. In some cases, repair is only part of the fix and a replacement is the cleaner answer.
