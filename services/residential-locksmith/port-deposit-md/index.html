Residential locksmith in Port Deposit - common questions

I just moved into a house in Port Deposit. Should I rekey the locks or replace them?

If the existing locks are in good shape, rekeying is usually the cleaner choice because it changes who can open the door without changing the hardware. If the lock is worn, sticky, missing parts, or doesn't match the rest of the door, replacement can make more sense. A quick look at the hardware and the door condition usually settles it.

What should I have ready before you come out to work on my home locks?

It helps to know which doors are involved, what the lock is doing, and whether the door has been repaired before. If you still have an old key, bring it. If the lock is on a storm door, side door, or mailbox, say that up front. That saves guesswork and helps make sure the right parts are on hand.

My old front door in Port Deposit sticks. Will a new deadbolt fix that, or is the door the real problem?

Sometimes the lock is the issue, but on older doors the frame, hinges, or strike plate can be the real cause. If the door is sagging or the latch is scraping, a new deadbolt alone may not solve it. The lock should be fitted to a door that closes properly, or it will keep giving you trouble.

Is a smart lock a good idea for a stone house on Main Street, or should I stay with a key lock?

A smart lock can work well if the door is sound and the hardware fits correctly. On older homes, the bigger question is whether the door lines up well enough for the lock to operate smoothly. If the frame has shifted or the trim is tight, a traditional deadbolt may be the better fit until the door is corrected.

Can you replace just the mailbox lock if the key is gone and the box itself is still fine?

Yes. If the mailbox body is in good condition, the lock cylinder can usually be replaced without changing the whole box. That's common when the key is lost, the lock is worn out, or the mailbox was left by a previous owner. If the door or housing is bent, then the fix may need more than the lock.
