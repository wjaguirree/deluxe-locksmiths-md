Lock Repair in Hockessin - common questions

Why does my front door lock in Hockessin feel stiff only when the door is shut?

That usually points to alignment. The lock may be fine, but the latch and strike aren't meeting cleanly when the door closes. Seasonal movement, a sagging hinge, or a shifted strike plate can all cause that. We inspect the door, not just the lock body, so we can correct the real cause instead of replacing usable parts.

What should I have ready before you come to repair a lock on my house?

If you can, tell us which door is giving trouble, what the lock does when you turn the key, and whether the key has ever been forced. It also helps to know if the lock is part of a keyed-alike setup for a front door, gate, or outbuilding. That information helps us plan the repair more accurately.

Can a worn lock be repaired, or does it always need to be replaced?

Not always. Many locks can be repaired if the issue is wear, misalignment, or a failed internal part that can be adjusted or replaced. If the housing is cracked, the mechanism is badly damaged, or the hardware no longer matches the door, replacement may be the better option. We'll explain what still makes sense.

My key turns partway but the deadbolt won't retract. Is that a lock problem or a door problem?

It can be either, and sometimes both. A binding deadbolt may be caused by the lock cylinder, the tailpiece, the strike plate, or the door shifting against the frame. We check the full setup before changing parts, because fixing only the visible symptom often leaves the real problem behind.

Can you repair one lock while keeping the same key for the rest of my doors and gate?

Often, yes, if the hardware is compatible and the keying system allows it. On properties with multiple entries, we look at how the lock fits into the existing setup before making changes. If the lock is restricted-key or part of a matched system, we'll explain what can stay keyed alike and what can't.
