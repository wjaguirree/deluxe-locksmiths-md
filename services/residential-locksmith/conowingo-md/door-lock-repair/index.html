Door Lock Repair in Conowingo - common questions

Why does my front door in Conowingo only lock if I lift or push on it?

That usually means the latch and strike plate are out of alignment, or the hinges have loosened and the door has dropped. We check the hardware first, then adjust or repair the parts that control how the bolt meets the frame. If the lock itself is worn, we can replace that piece instead of changing the whole door set.

What should we have ready before you come out to repair a door lock?

It helps if someone can show us the problem door, the key that's used on it, and whether the issue happens every time or only when the weather changes. If the door has been forced, swollen, or repaired before, that matters too. We can work from there and choose the right hardware fix.

Is it a problem if the deadbolt works but the latch feels rough on my house near the Susquehanna?

Yes, that usually means one part of the door set is wearing faster than the other. A rough latch can mean a bent strike, a dirty or worn latch body, or a door that has shifted. Leaving it alone can make the problem worse, so we check the entire closing edge and repair what's causing the drag.

Should we repair the lock or replace it on a cabin off US-1?

If the lock is solid and the trouble is mainly alignment, repair is often the better choice. If the cylinder is worn, the bolt is damaged, or the case is cracked inside the door, replacement makes more sense. We'll look at the hardware, explain what failed, and fit the fix to the condition of the door.

Can you fix a lock if the key still turns but the door won't catch all the way?

Usually, yes. That symptom often points to a latch that isn't entering the strike cleanly, not a full lock failure. We can realign the parts, repair worn components, or replace the damaged piece if needed. If the frame has shifted or the door is swollen, we handle that side of the problem too.
