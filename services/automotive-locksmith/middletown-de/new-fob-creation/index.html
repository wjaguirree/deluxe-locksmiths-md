New Fob Creation in Middletown - common questions

How do you make a new fob if I don't have any working one left?

We identify the vehicle, get the correct replacement fob, and program it directly to the car. For many vehicles, that means working through the lock system and onboard electronics instead of cloning an existing fob. If the car has special anti-theft steps or requires extra setup, we'll explain what's needed before we start.

What should I have ready when you come out to Middletown for a new fob?

Have the vehicle available, the registration if you have it, and a way to confirm you're the owner or authorized driver. If the car is locked, stuck in park, or has warning lights on, tell us that up front. Small details like the model year and whether it's push-to-start help us bring the right replacement.

Is it a security risk to keep driving with only one fob left?

It can be. If the last fob is lost, stolen, or damaged, you may be left with no backup and no way to protect the car from someone who finds the old one. In some cases we can also help with reprogramming so missing fobs are no longer accepted by the vehicle, which is a smart step after a loss.

Should I replace the fob only, or do I need the car reprogrammed too?

If a fob is damaged but still matched to the vehicle, a replacement alone may be enough. If the fob is lost, stolen, or you want an old one removed from use, reprogramming matters. We'll look at the vehicle's setup and tell you whether a straight replacement will do the job or whether the old fob should be cleared.

What if my car is at a Route 299 shopping center and the fob is locked inside?

That can still be handled, but the right fix depends on whether the issue is a lockout, a dead fob, or both. We can open the vehicle and then deal with the fob problem if needed. If the fob is completely gone, we can also source and program a new one once the car is accessible.
