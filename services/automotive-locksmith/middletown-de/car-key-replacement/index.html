Car Key Replacement in Middletown - common questions

How do we know if my car needs a duplicate key or a full replacement in Middletown?

If you still have a working key, a duplicate may be enough. If every copy is gone, the key is broken beyond use, or the car won't recognize the key, we may need to replace and program it. We look at the vehicle, the key type, and whether the problem is the blade, the chip, or the fob itself.

What should I have ready when we call about my car key in Middletown, DE?

Have the vehicle year, make, model, and VIN ready if you can. We also need proof that the car belongs to you or that you're allowed to request the work. If you have any working key, bring it along. If the key is stuck, broken, or locked in the car, tell us that up front so we know what tools and parts to bring.

My key opens the door but won't start the car. Is that a key problem or a car problem?

It can be either, but a key that opens the doors and won't start the engine often points to a transponder or programming issue. A weak fob battery or a damaged chip can do that. If the dash shows a key warning or the car acts like the key isn't there, we can sort out whether the key needs replacement or the vehicle needs a different fix.

If I have two different keys for my car, how do we know which one to replace?

We check which key is failing and what each one is doing. One may still start the car while the other only works in the lock, or one may be a valet key and the other the main key. If both are worn, it may make more sense to replace the one you use most and keep the better backup.

Can you replace my key if it's locked in the car at a Route 299 shopping center?

Yes, if the car can be opened and the vehicle details check out, we can often help with both the lockout and the replacement. That matters when the key is gone, not just out of reach. We'll need the car information and proof you're allowed to request service before we start working on the vehicle.
