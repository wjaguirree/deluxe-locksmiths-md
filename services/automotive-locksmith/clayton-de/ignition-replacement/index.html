Ignition Replacement in Clayton - common questions

How do you know if my Clayton car needs ignition replacement instead of just a new key?

If the key turns poorly, sticks, or the car only starts after repeated tries, the ignition may be worn even when the key still looks fine. We check how the key enters, turns, and releases, then compare that with the vehicle's symptoms. If the switch or cylinder has failed, a new key alone usually won't fix it.

What should I have ready when I call about my ignition in Clayton?

Have the vehicle year, make, and model ready, plus whether the key is stuck, the steering wheel is locked, or the car will not recognize the key. If the car has been modified or the ignition was already worked on, tell us that too. That helps us bring the right parts and plan the job correctly.

Can a bad ignition leave me locked out of my car in a lot near the tracks?

Yes. A worn ignition can keep the key from turning, and some cars also lock the steering or shifter in place. If that happens after a lockout, we first get the vehicle accessible and then inspect the ignition hardware. The goal is to separate a lock problem from an ignition failure so we can address the real issue.

Should I replace the whole ignition or try to keep using my old key?

If the existing key still matches the vehicle well, we try to key the replacement ignition to that key when the vehicle allows it. That keeps things simple. If the key or cylinder is too worn, a fresh match may be the better path. We'll explain which option fits the car once we see it.

What if my key starts the car sometimes but not every time?

That's a common sign of wear in the ignition switch, the cylinder, or both. The problem may show up only when the wheel is turned, the shifter is moved, or the key is wiggled. We can inspect the setup and replace the failed parts if needed. Waiting usually makes the failure more unpredictable, not less.
