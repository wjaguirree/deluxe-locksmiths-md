Automotive locksmith in Rising Sun - common questions

Can you make a new car key if mine is gone and the vehicle is parked in Rising Sun?

Yes. If the vehicle is on site, a new key can often be made and programmed there instead of moving the car. The exact process depends on the make, model, and whether it uses a standard key, chip key, or smart fob. If the original is missing, expect us to verify the vehicle and the needed key type first.

What should I have ready before you come out to my car or truck?

Have the vehicle make, model, and year ready, plus whatever key, fob, or broken piece you still have. If the car is at home, a job site, or a lot, make sure the vehicle is accessible and you can prove it's yours. If there's a stuck key or a broken ignition, don't force it before help arrives.

Is it a bad idea to keep using a key that's already sticking in the ignition?

Yes, because a sticking key often means wear inside the key or the ignition cylinder. Forcing it can leave the key broken off or damage the cylinder further. In some cases a new key solves it, but if the lock is worn, the ignition may need repair or replacement to keep the problem from coming back.

Should I try to replace the fob battery myself or is programming usually part of the job?

A battery swap can help if the fob only has weak range or intermittent response. But if the vehicle isn't recognizing the fob at all, battery replacement may not be enough. Many cars need the fob programmed to the vehicle, and some need a new fob created from scratch if the old one is damaged or lost.

What if my key broke off in the ignition while I was leaving the town square or parked on a farm lane?

That can usually be handled on site. The broken piece needs to be removed without pushing it deeper or scratching the cylinder. After that, the key and ignition should be checked together, because a broken key often points to a worn lock or a bent key that could fail again if nothing else is addressed.
