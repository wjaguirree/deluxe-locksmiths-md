New Fob Creation in Fallston - common questions

How does new fob creation work if I have no working fob left?

We identify the vehicle, source the correct replacement fob, and program it to the car from scratch. If the system allows it, we can also remove lost remotes from memory so they no longer match the vehicle. The exact steps depend on the make, model, and security system.

What should I have ready when you come out to Fallston for a lost fob?

Have the vehicle, proof that it's yours, and any key details you still know, like the year, make, and model. If the car is parked behind a gate, in a barn area, or at a long driveway off the road, make sure we can reach it safely. That keeps the job moving without extra back-and-forth.

What's the security risk if I keep using the car after the fob is missing?

If the lost fob is still out there, someone may be able to get into the vehicle if it's a proximity system. Even when the car still runs, you can end up leaving it less secure than you think. Reprogramming with a new fob can help restore control and reduce that risk.

Do I need a brand-new fob, or can an older spare be used instead?

Sometimes a spare can be programmed if it matches the vehicle and is still compatible. Other times the old remote is too damaged, wrong for the car, or no longer recognized by the system. We'll tell you which route fits the vehicle once we check the setup and what hardware is available.

Can you make a new fob for a car that's stuck at home in Fallston?

Yes. This is usually handled where the car is parked, which helps when the vehicle can't be driven or opened normally. That matters on properties where the car sits well off the road, near a workshop, or out by the Deer Creek valley side of town, where getting the vehicle to a shop isn't practical.
