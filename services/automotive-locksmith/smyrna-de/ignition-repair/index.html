Other times the steering wheel locks up against the cylinder, or the car starts one day and sticks the next. Those are mechanical warning signs, not just a fussy key.

Ignition Repair in Smyrna - common questions

My key turns hard in the ignition in Smyrna. How do you figure out whether it's the key or the cylinder?

We check both. A worn key can mimic a bad ignition, and a worn cylinder can make a good key feel rough. We look for sticking, binding, and uneven wear before touching anything. If the key profile is the real issue, replacing the key may solve it. If the cylinder is worn, it needs repair or replacement.

What should I have ready before you work on my ignition?

Have the vehicle make, model, and year handy, plus any spare keys you have. If the problem is intermittent, it helps to describe exactly when it happens, like starting cold, after parking on a hill, or when the wheel is turned. If the key is bent, cracked, or heavily worn, show that too.

Can forcing a sticky ignition make the repair worse?

Yes. Forcing it can break the key, damage the cylinder wafers, or make the steering lock bind harder. That often turns a repairable ignition into a more involved problem. If the key is already fighting you, it's better to stop using pressure and have it checked before anything snaps inside the column.

Is it better to repair the ignition cylinder or replace it?

That depends on the wear and the vehicle. If the cylinder is dirty or mildly worn, repair may be enough. If the internal parts are badly damaged, replacement is the safer choice. We try to save what still works, but we won't recommend a patch if it's likely to leave you stuck again.

My car key is stuck after I turned it off at a shopping center on US-13. Can you still help if the key won't come out?

Often, yes. A key that won't release can be caused by the shifter position, a worn cylinder, or a problem in the ignition lock itself. Don't yank on it or twist harder. That can bend the key or jam the cylinder. The right fix starts with finding what's holding it in place.
