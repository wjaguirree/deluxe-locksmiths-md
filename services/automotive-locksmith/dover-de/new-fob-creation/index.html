New Fob Creation in Dover - common questions

How does new fob creation work when my car has no working fob left in Dover?

We identify the vehicle, bring the correct replacement fob, cut any backup key if the car uses one, and program everything at the vehicle. With no working fob left, the order matters: we confirm the right part first, then pair it, then test every function so you know the car recognizes it.

What should I have ready before you meet me near The Green or anywhere else in Dover?

Have the vehicle with you, proof that you own or are authorized to use it, and any remaining key pieces or broken fob parts if you still have them. If the car has been sitting, tell us that up front. Battery issues, prior attempts at programming, or aftermarket alarms can change the setup.

Could trying to start the car without a fob make the problem worse?

Usually it won't damage the car just because the fob is missing, but repeated failed attempts or a weak vehicle battery can complicate programming. If the car has already had a lot of unsuccessful tries, tell us before we begin so we can check for anything that would block the new fob from pairing correctly.

Should I replace the old fob shell or create a brand-new fob for my car?

If the electronics still work and only the shell is cracked, a shell replacement may be enough. If the fob is dead, lost, water-damaged, or the car has no working copy left, a brand-new fob is the better path. We'll look at the condition first, then match the fix to the vehicle.

What if my car is stuck in a lot in Dover and I can't open it at all?

That can still be handled in the field. We first get the vehicle open, then identify the right fob and program it after access is restored. If the battery is dead or the car has an added security system, that can affect the order of work, but it doesn't automatically stop the job.
