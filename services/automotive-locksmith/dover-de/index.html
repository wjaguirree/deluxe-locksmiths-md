Modern car locks are a mix of mechanical parts and electronics. The metal blade still has to match the door and ignition, but most Dover vehicles also rely on a transponder chip or a smart fob that the car has to recognize before it will start. When a key wears down, a fob stops talking to the vehicle, or an ignition cylinder gets sticky, the problem is usually in the hardware, not just the button or battery.

That's why automotive locksmith work has to fit the car you actually drive, not a generic blank from a parts shelf. Automotive locksmith in Dover - common questions

My key turns in the door but the car still won't start in Dover. What usually failed?

That usually points to the transponder chip, the fob, or the ignition hardware rather than the metal key cut itself. The blade may still open the door, but the car needs the chip or fob to be recognized. On some vehicles, a worn ignition cylinder can also keep the key from reading or turning correctly.

What should I have ready before you come to my car in Dover?

Have the vehicle make, model, and year ready, plus proof that you're allowed to have the car serviced. If you still have one working key or fob, bring it out if you can. That helps with duplication, programming, and checking whether the problem is the key itself or the car's lock or ignition.

Can trying to force a stuck key in the ignition make things worse?

Yes. Forcing it can bend the key, damage the cylinder wafers, or leave part of the key broken off inside. If the key is already hard to turn, stop using it and let the ignition be checked before the damage spreads. A damaged cylinder can turn a small issue into a full replacement.

Should I replace the whole fob, or can a new battery or programming fix it?

If the shell is intact and the electronics are still healthy, a battery or programming may be enough. If the buttons are broken, the case is cracked, or the internal board failed, a new fob makes more sense. The right answer depends on whether the problem is the case, the battery, or the chip inside.

If my only key is lost in Dover, can you still make a new one without towing the car?

In many cases, yes. A locksmith can often work from the vehicle's lock or ignition information, then cut and program a replacement on site. Some vehicles are more locked down than others, so the exact process depends on the model, but losing the only key does not always mean a tow or dealership visit.
