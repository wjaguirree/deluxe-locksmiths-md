How does new fob creation work when I don't have the old one in Odessa?

We identify the vehicle, bring the correct new fob, and program it to the car so it can recognize it as an authorized device. If the old fob is gone, we work from the vehicle's system and the make and model details. The goal is a fob that starts the car and handles the functions it was built to use.

What should I have ready before you make a new fob for my car?

Have the vehicle nearby, plus any registration or proof that helps confirm it's your car. If you still have a working key or an old fob shell, bring that too. Even if everything is lost, we can still often help, but the vehicle information has to be right so we source the correct fob.

Can a wrong fob be programmed by mistake, or is that just a bad parts problem?

Both can happen. A fob can look close enough to fool someone, but still be the wrong system or wrong frequency for the car. If the wrong unit is used, the vehicle may not accept it, or only part of the functions may work. A proper job starts with the exact vehicle match, not the appearance of the fob.

Should I replace the lost fob with the same kind, or switch to a different one?

That depends on what the vehicle supports. If your car uses a basic remote, a transponder key, or a push-button fob, we match the replacement to the system it already has. We don't push a fancier setup just because it's newer. The best choice is the one that works cleanly with your car and the way you use it.
