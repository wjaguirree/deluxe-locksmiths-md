Car Key Duplication in Newark - common questions

How does car key duplication work for a newer vehicle in Newark?

We start by checking the key type and the vehicle's security setup. Some newer cars need a cut key plus programming so the spare is accepted by the car. Others only need a blade copy. We match the process to the vehicle, then test the spare so it does what it should before we wrap up.

What should I have ready when I call about a spare car key?

Have the vehicle make, model, and year ready, plus proof that you're allowed to get a key made for it. If you still have one working key, bring that too. If the original is badly worn or damaged, let us know. That helps us figure out whether a straight duplication will work or whether the key needs extra attention.

What happens if I wait until I lose my only car key?

That can turn a simple backup into a much bigger problem. Without a spare, the car may be stuck where it is until a replacement can be made and programmed. It can also leave you without a way to move the car if the original is lost, broken, or locked inside. A duplicate gives you a fallback before that happens.

Should I get a regular spare key or a remote key copy for my car?

It depends on the vehicle and how you use it. Some drivers only need a basic backup that starts the car. Others want the lock and unlock functions too. We can look at the key you have now and explain what kind of duplicate makes sense for your setup, without adding features you won't use.

Can you make a duplicate if my original car key is worn or bent?

Sometimes, yes, but a badly worn key can be hard to copy accurately. If the cuts are damaged or the chip is failing, we may need to work from the vehicle information instead of relying on the original alone. We'll be straight with you about what can be duplicated and what needs a different approach.
