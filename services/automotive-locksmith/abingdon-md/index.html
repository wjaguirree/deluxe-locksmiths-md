Automotive locksmith in Abingdon - common questions

If my car is stuck in an Abingdon parking lot, do you make the new key there?

Yes. For most automotive locksmith jobs, the work is done where the vehicle sits. That includes cutting a replacement key, programming a fob, or handling an ignition issue on site. If the car can't be started or moved, there's usually no need to tow it to a dealership just to solve the key problem.

What should I have ready before I call about a lost car key in Abingdon?

Have the vehicle make, model, year, and the exact location ready. If you still have one working key or fob, mention that too. It also helps to know whether the car uses a metal key, a transponder key, or a push-button start system, since that changes the work needed.

My fob unlocks the doors but the car won't start. Is that usually the fob or the ignition?

Either one can be the problem. On many newer vehicles, the fob may lock and unlock the doors while the transponder part fails to communicate with the car. On older vehicles, the ignition cylinder or key wear may be the issue. A proper diagnosis keeps you from replacing the wrong part.

Should I replace the key, the fob, or both for a car in Abingdon?

It depends on the vehicle and the failure. If the metal key is worn, duplication or replacement may solve it. If the remote functions are dead but the car still starts, the fob may need programming or a new shell or battery. If the vehicle uses a combined smart key, both functions may be tied together.

Can you remove a broken key from the ignition without replacing the whole ignition?

Often, yes. If the key snapped and the cylinder itself is still serviceable, key extraction can be enough. If the ignition is worn, seized, or damaged from repeated sticking, repair or replacement may be the better fix. The condition of the cylinder has to be checked before deciding.
