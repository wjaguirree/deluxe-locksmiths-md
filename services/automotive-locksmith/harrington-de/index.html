Most automotive lock and key problems in Harrington look alike at first, but they usually come from different parts of the system. If the door unlocks but the car won't start, the issue may be the transponder chip, the fob, or the ignition, not the mechanical key cut. If the remote still locks the doors but won't start the car, a new fob or programming may be needed, while a dead battery in the fob can make a good key seem bad.

Automotive locksmith in Harrington - common questions

My car unlocks but won't start in Harrington. Is that a key problem or an ignition problem?

It could be either. If the key blade turns normally but the engine won't recognize the key, the transponder or fob may be the issue. If the key feels rough, sticks, or won't rotate fully, the ignition cylinder may be worn. We check the key, the fob, and the ignition before replacing parts.

What should I have ready when you come out for a car key issue?

Have the vehicle registration and a photo ID if you can, since that helps confirm the car belongs to you. If you have a spare key or fob, bring that too. It also helps to know the make, model, and year, because programming and key styles vary by vehicle.

I lost my only key near the fairgrounds. Is there a risk in trying to start the car with another key type?

Yes. Forcing the wrong key or a damaged copy can wear the ignition or snap the key off inside it. If the vehicle uses a chip key or smart fob, the right cut alone may not start it anyway. We can make and program the correct replacement at the vehicle.

How do I know whether I need a new fob or just a duplicate key?

If the remote buttons work but the car won't start, you may need programming or a transponder check. If the blade works in the door and ignition, but you want another copy for backup, duplication may be enough. Some vehicles use a plain mechanical key, while others need both cutting and programming.

My key broke off in the ignition in Harrington. Can that be fixed without replacing everything?

Often, yes. If the cylinder itself is still sound, we can extract the broken piece and inspect the key and ignition before deciding on the next step. If the cylinder is damaged or worn, replacement may be the better repair. We'll tell you what the part is doing, not just replace it blindly.
