How does car key duplication work if my car has a chip key?

We copy the blade cut, then program the electronic part if your vehicle uses one. Some keys only need cutting, while others need both steps to work properly. We match the duplicate to the vehicle you drive and test it before we wrap up so you know the door, ignition, and remote functions are behaving the way they should.

What should I have ready when you come to make a spare key in Harrington?

Have the vehicle available and, if possible, the working key you want copied. If you have the registration or another way to show the car belongs to you, that helps us verify the job and choose the right blank. If the original key is worn or damaged, let us know so we can judge whether it is still a good source to copy from.

Why is it a problem to keep driving with only one car key?

Because one mistake can stop the car from being usable. If that only key is lost, broken, or locked inside the vehicle, you may be left waiting on a rescue instead of driving away. It can also put you in a tougher security position, since you have no backup if the key starts failing or disappears.

Should I duplicate my worn key now or wait until I get a new one?

If the original still works, it is usually better to make the spare sooner rather than later. A badly worn key can still be copied, but if the cuts are too rounded off, the duplicate may carry over the same problem. In that case, we may recommend using the vehicle data or another working key, if one exists, as the better source.
