Ignition Replacement in Claymont - common questions

How does ignition replacement work if my key still turns sometimes in Claymont?

If the key only works part of the time, we check whether the problem is the key, the cylinder, or the ignition switch itself. Sometimes a worn key is fooling you into thinking the ignition is bad. Other times the cylinder is failing and the key is just along for the ride. We replace the failed part and match it to your existing key when that can be done.

What should I have ready before you come out to my car near Philadelphia Pike?

Have the vehicle year, make, model, and your current key or fob ready. If you have a spare key, that helps us compare how the system behaves. It's also useful to know whether the steering wheel locked up, whether the key was hard to remove, or whether the problem started after a battery issue or a rough turn of the key.

Can forcing my key damage the ignition in my car?

Yes. Forcing a sticking key can bend the key, break a piece off inside the cylinder, or wear the ignition farther out of line. If the key is catching, stop and don't keep twisting it. A worn key or a failing cylinder can turn a simple repair into a larger one if it's pushed too hard.

Should I replace the ignition or just get a new key for my vehicle in Claymont?

If the key is worn but the ignition is still healthy, a new key may solve it. If the cylinder is sticking, not recognizing the key, or failing to return properly, ignition replacement is the better fix. We look at the symptom pattern first so you're not replacing parts that don't need replacing.

What if my car won't move because the ignition is locked and it's parked by the Claymont rail station?

That can happen with a failed ignition or a steering lock problem. We can usually service the vehicle where it sits if there's enough room to work safely. If the ignition is jammed so tightly that the car can't be handled without causing damage, we'll talk through the next step before touching it.
