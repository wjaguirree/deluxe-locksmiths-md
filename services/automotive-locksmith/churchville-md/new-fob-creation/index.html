New Fob Creation in Churchville - common questions

How do you make a new car fob if I don't have the original one anymore?

We identify the vehicle, bring the correct replacement fob, open the car if needed, and program the new unit to the security system from scratch. Some vehicles allow straightforward onboard programming. Others need additional steps. Either way, the job starts with matching the right fob to the car, not guessing.

What should I have ready when I call for a new fob in Churchville?

Have the vehicle year, make, model, and if possible the VIN. If you're locked out, let us know that too. It also helps to tell us whether the fob is lost, broken, or dead, because those details affect how we handle the programming and entry.

Is there any risk that a new fob won't work on my truck or SUV?

Yes, especially on newer vehicles or models with stronger anti-theft systems. If the wrong part is used, it won't program correctly. Some vehicles also won't accept new fobs unless the car is accessible and the correct security procedure is followed. That's why matching the exact system matters.

Should I replace the battery first or just get a whole new fob?

If the fob still unlocks the car and the case is sound, a battery issue may be all it needs. If the fob is missing, broken, water-damaged, or won't communicate with the car at all, a full replacement is usually the better path. We can tell which situation you're dealing with once we see it.

Can you still help if the car is locked and the fob is totally dead in a lot near MD-22?

Yes. That's a common setup for this kind of work. We can open the vehicle first, then move into fob creation and programming once we have access. If the car's security system needs the doors open or the ignition accessible, getting in first is part of the process.
