New Fob Creation in Charlestown - common questions

If I have no working fob left, how do you make a new one for my car in Charlestown?

We start by identifying the vehicle and the exact fob it needs. Then we source a new one and pair it to the car from scratch. If the system allows it, we can set up the new fob on site so the vehicle recognizes it for locking, unlocking, and starting.

What should I have ready before you come out to make a new car fob?

Have the vehicle itself, proof you're allowed to have it, and the make, model, and year if you know them. If there's a spare key or old fob hidden somewhere, tell us that too. Small details matter because the wrong fob won't program correctly.

Can you still help if the only fob was lost in the water or left somewhere around the marina?

Yes. If the original fob is gone for good, we don't need to copy it first. We can work from the vehicle and the correct replacement fob. If the car is locked and the trailer is still attached, we can deal with that setup as part of the job.

Do I need a whole new key, or can you make just the fob for my vehicle?

That depends on the car. Some vehicles use a separate fob and key, while others use an integrated style. We'll match the right part to the vehicle and program it properly. The goal is to get you a working setup that fits how your car was built.

What if my car is older or has an unusual fob setup?

Some older vehicles and some specialty models use systems that are less straightforward, and a few can't be handled the same way as newer cars. We can usually tell you what's possible once we know the vehicle details. If the right replacement can't be sourced or programmed, we'll say so plainly.
