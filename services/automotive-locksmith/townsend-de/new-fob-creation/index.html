What should I have ready when you come out to Blackbird or along Route 71?

Have the vehicle, the VIN if you know it, and proof that it's yours. If there's a hidden valet key, old remote shell, or any paperwork from the car, bring that too. It helps us identify the exact fob type and keeps the job moving in the right direction.

Can a damaged fob be risky to keep using if the buttons still work sometimes?

Yes. A cracked case, weak battery contacts, or water damage can make the fob unreliable, and the problem usually gets worse. If the chip or board fails at the wrong time, you can lose remote functions or even the ability to start the car. Replacing it before it fails completely is the safer route.

Should I replace just the shell or get a whole new fob for my Townsend vehicle?

If the electronics still read correctly and only the shell is broken, a shell swap may be enough. If the board is damaged, the battery contacts are worn out, or the chip is missing, a full new fob is the better choice. We check the hardware first so you're not fixing the wrong part.
