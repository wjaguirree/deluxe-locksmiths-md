Ignition Replacement in Edgewood - common questions

How do you replace an ignition on a car in Edgewood without towing it?

If the vehicle is accessible, the ignition hardware can usually be serviced where it sits. The old cylinder or switch is removed, the replacement is fitted, and the parts are tested before the job is wrapped up. Some vehicles also let the new ignition be matched to your current key, depending on the make and model.

What should I have ready before calling about an ignition problem in Edgewood?

Have the year, make, model, and if possible the VIN. If you still have the key, bring it along or keep it with the vehicle. It also helps to know what the car is doing now, such as whether the key turns, sticks, or comes out normally. That gives a clearer picture of whether the issue is the cylinder, switch, or both.

Can a worn key damage the ignition in my car?

Yes. A key that's bent, worn down, or cut poorly can make the cylinder work harder than it should. Over time, that can wear the tumblers or make the key stick. If the problem has been going on for a while, the ignition may need to be replaced instead of just being worked free.

Should I replace the ignition or just keep using the key I have?

If the key still turns smoothly and the issue is only the key itself, a new key may be enough. If the cylinder is sticking, the key won't turn reliably, or the switch is failing behind the cylinder, replacement is the better fix. The right answer depends on what part of the ignition assembly is actually worn.

What happens if my key is stuck in the ignition at the Edgewood MARC station lot?

That usually means the cylinder, shifter interlock, or release mechanism is not lining up the way it should. Don't force the key, since that can break it off or damage the cylinder further. The car needs to be inspected on site so the issue can be traced to the lock, the switch, or the surrounding hardware.
