New Fob Creation in Forest Hill - common questions

How do you make a new fob if I don't have the old one anymore?

We identify the exact vehicle and key system, source the correct new fob, and program it to the car as a fresh setup. If the vehicle uses a transponder or smart key, that has to be matched correctly too. The process is different from copying an existing fob, because the car has nothing to read from at the start.

What should I have ready when you come out for a lost fob in Forest Hill?

Have the vehicle accessible, the registration if you can get it, and proof that it's your car. If you know the year, make, model, and trim, that helps narrow the right fob. If the car is locked and the keys are inside, tell us that up front so we can plan for the lockout and the new fob work together.

Can a used fob be programmed, or does it need to be brand new?

Some vehicles will only accept a new fob, and some used ones have already been locked to another car. A used unit can be a gamble, especially when there's no working fob left to compare against. A proper new fob is the safer route when the goal is a clean, reliable result.

What usually goes wrong when people try to replace a fob themselves?

The most common problems are buying the wrong part, assuming the buttons are enough, or getting a remote that won't pair to the security system. Another issue is ending up with a fob that unlocks doors but won't allow the car to start. The fix is to match the exact vehicle setup before programming anything.

If my keys are locked in the car near Rocks State Park, can a new fob still be created?

Yes, if we can access the vehicle and identify the correct system, a new fob can be sourced and programmed even when the original set is locked inside. The important part is knowing the vehicle details and making sure the new fob is matched properly, not just opened into the car.
