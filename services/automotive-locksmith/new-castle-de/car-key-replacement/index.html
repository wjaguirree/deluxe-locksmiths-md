How does car key replacement work if I lost every key to my car in New Castle?

We identify the vehicle, cut a key that matches it, and program it so the car accepts it. If the car is locked, we can handle that part first. The exact process depends on the make, model, and key type, but the goal is the same: get you back to a key that starts the car and works the locks.

What should I have ready before you come out to replace my car key?

Have the vehicle make, model, year, and VIN if you can find it on the dash or paperwork. If you have any old key, remote, or key fob pieces, keep them with you. We also need to know whether the car is locked, parked in a lot, or sitting at home so we can plan the work around the access.

Can something go wrong if I try to order a key online first?

Yes. A lot of cars use key blanks and electronics that have to match the vehicle exactly. Ordering the wrong blank or remote can leave you with a part that won't start the car. If you're unsure, it's better to identify the vehicle first and have us match the right key to it before any cutting or programming.

Should I get a spare key made too, or just replace the one I lost?

If the car only has one working key left, making a spare is usually the safer move. Once the replacement is done, we can often duplicate it so you aren't stuck again later. If every key is gone, we replace the lost one first and then talk about a backup once the car is running.
