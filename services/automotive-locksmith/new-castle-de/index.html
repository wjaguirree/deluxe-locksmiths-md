Automotive locksmith in New Castle - common questions

How do you make a new car key in New Castle if I don't have the original?

We can often make a replacement by working from the vehicle itself, then programming the transponder or fob so it matches the car. The exact process depends on the make, model, and year. Some vehicles only need a cut key, while others need electronic setup before they'll start.

What should I have ready when you come out for my car in New Castle?

Have the vehicle's make, model, year, and the location of the car ready. If you have proof that it's your vehicle, keep that nearby too. If the problem is a lost key, broken fob, or stuck ignition, let us know what the car is doing so we can bring the right equipment.

Can trying to turn a stuck ignition make things worse?

It can. Forcing the key can break it off in the cylinder or wear the ignition further. If the key is hard to turn, stuck, or only works some of the time, stop before it snaps. We can look at the key, cylinder, and surrounding parts to decide whether repair, extraction, or replacement is the better fix.

Should I replace my fob or try to repair the key first?

If the blade is worn but the electronics still work, a new key cut or a fresh duplicate may be enough. If the buttons, battery contacts, or chip are failing, a new fob may make more sense. We look at the condition of the key and the vehicle's system before recommending one path over the other.

My key snapped off in the ignition near Delaware Street. Can that still be fixed on site?

Often, yes. We can remove broken key pieces from the ignition and inspect the cylinder for damage. If the cylinder is worn or the key broke because the ignition is failing, we can talk through repair or replacement. The key point is not to keep twisting what's left of the key.
