If I lost every fob for my car in Hockessin, can you still make a new one?

In many cases, yes. We can source a new fob, cut any required blade, and program it to the vehicle even when there isn't a working copy left. The exact process depends on the make, model, year, and security system, so we'll need the vehicle details and proof that it's yours.

What should I have ready before you come out for a new fob?

Have the vehicle identification details, registration or title information, and a clear spot to work around the car. If you've got any remaining key parts, remote shell, or the owner's manual, keep them handy. It also helps to know whether the car is in a garage, behind a gate, or blocked in.

Can trying a bunch of online steps make my car harder to program?

It can. Some vehicles limit repeated failed programming attempts or react badly to the wrong procedure. If the fob is gone and you're not sure of the exact system, it's better to stop and let us handle it. That keeps us from chasing a problem that was created by guesswork.

Do I need a replacement fob that matches the exact one I lost?

Usually it needs to match the vehicle, not just look identical. The button layout, chip type, and remote functions all have to fit the car's system. If you're unsure, send us the year, make, and model, and we'll sort out the correct option before we program anything.

What if the only fob I have is damaged but the car still starts sometimes?

That's the kind of warning sign worth dealing with before it fails completely. A weak or damaged fob may work off and on for a while, but it can leave you stranded without warning. We can often replace it and set up the new one so you're not relying on something that's already failing.
