Automotive locksmith in Hockessin - common questions

My car key works sometimes and then won't start the car in Hockessin. What do you check first?

We start with the key or fob itself, then check the vehicle's reader, battery power, and ignition system if needed. Intermittent problems can come from a weak fob battery, a worn transponder chip, damaged key blades, or a failing ignition cylinder. We look at the whole setup before deciding whether programming, repair, or replacement makes sense.

What should I have ready when you come out to make a replacement car key or fob?

Have the vehicle accessible, and have proof that it's yours. If you still have a working key or fob, bring that along too, since it can help us match the right blank or program a new one. If the car has an alarm, aftermarket remote start, or unusual ignition work, mentioning that ahead of time helps us plan.

Can a dead key fob near a long driveway be fixed, or does it always need a new one?

Not always. Sometimes the fob only needs a battery, sometimes it needs to be reprogrammed, and sometimes the internal board is damaged and replacement is the right answer. If the vehicle is reading the fob poorly, we can sort out whether the problem is the fob, the car, or both before moving forward.

Should I replace my ignition or just have it repaired if the key is getting stuck?

If the key sticks because of wear, debris, or a damaged cylinder, repair may be enough. If the ignition is badly worn, binds often, or has already been forced, replacement can be the better long-term fix. We inspect the part in place and explain what the wear pattern means so you can decide with real information.

My car is older and my spouse's is a newer push-button model. Do those need the same kind of service?

No. Older vehicles often need cutting, transponder work, or ignition repair, while newer push-button vehicles may need fob programming, smart key creation, or a more involved diagnostic step. In Hockessin, we see both types often, and the right service depends on the system in your specific vehicle, not just the make and model.
