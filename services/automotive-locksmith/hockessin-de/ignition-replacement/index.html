Ignition Replacement in Hockessin - common questions

What happens during an ignition replacement on my car in Hockessin?

We check whether the issue is the ignition cylinder, the switch, or the key itself, then replace the failed part as needed. If the vehicle allows it, we can often key the new ignition to the key you already use. That keeps the setup simple and avoids changing more of the car than necessary.

What should I have ready before you come out for my ignition problem?

Have the vehicle key, any spare key, and the registration or proof that you're allowed to have the car worked on. Keep the car in park and don't keep trying to twist a stuck key. If the vehicle is behind a gate or down a long drive, make the path clear so we can access it without extra back-and-forth.

Can forcing the key damage the ignition?

Yes. Forcing a worn key can bend it, break the tip off in the cylinder, or damage the internal tumblers. If the key is already sticking, stop trying to turn it. A broken key inside the ignition can turn a simple replacement into a bigger repair.

Should I replace the ignition or just keep using my spare key?

If the spare works but the main key hangs up, the issue may be the key pattern. If both keys stick, the ignition is probably worn. We look at both before replacing parts. In some cases, the new ignition can be matched to your current key, which is usually the cleanest option.

My fob died while I was parked at the top of a long driveway. Is that still an ignition issue?

It can be. A dead fob can keep the car from recognizing the key, but some vehicles also have an ignition or reader problem that shows up at the same time. We'll sort out whether the trouble is in the fob, the transponder key, or the ignition system so you're not replacing the wrong part.
