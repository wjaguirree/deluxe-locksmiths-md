Can you make a spare car key from the one I already have in Hockessin?

Yes, if the key is still readable and the vehicle accepts a copied key, we can duplicate it. That can mean a plain cut key, a transponder key, or a fob with a programmed chip. If the original is worn badly, we'll check whether duplication is still clean enough or whether replacement makes more sense.

What should I have ready before we duplicate my car key near Lancaster Pike?

Have the vehicle with you, the working key if you still have one, and whatever proof shows you're allowed to have the key made. If the car uses a remote or chip key, bring every working fob you have so we can match the right setup. That keeps the job straightforward.

Is there any risk that copying my fob will make the original stop working?

Not when the job is done the right way. A normal duplicate should not erase or weaken the original key. The main issue is compatibility: some vehicles need the key cut and programmed in a specific way, and some badly worn keys should be replaced instead of copied.

Should I repair my car key, rekey the lock, or replace the key if it died at my house in Hockessin?

If the blade is damaged or the shell is cracked, repair may be enough. If the electronic part is failing or the key is lost, replacement is usually the better answer. Rekeying is for the lock cylinders themselves, not for bringing a dead car key back to life.

Can you still help if my only fob quit at the top of a long driveway and I have no spare?

Yes. That's a common reason people call us. If the vehicle is accessible, we can look at the key issue on site and figure out whether the best fix is repair, duplication, or a full replacement. The exact answer depends on the vehicle and the condition of the key system.
