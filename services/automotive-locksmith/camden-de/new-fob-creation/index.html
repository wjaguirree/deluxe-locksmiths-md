New Fob Creation in Camden - common questions

If I have no working fob at all, how do you make a new one in Camden?

We identify the vehicle, source the correct replacement fob, and program it so the car accepts it as an authorized device. If the original fob is gone completely, we work from the vehicle's security system rather than trying to duplicate an existing remote. Some vehicles are simpler than others, but the process always depends on the exact make, model, and trim.

What should I have ready before you come out for a new fob?

Have the vehicle year, make, model, and if possible the VIN. If you have any remaining key or emergency insert, keep that with you. It also helps to know where the car is parked and whether it's fully locked or sitting with the keys inside. That information lets us bring the right replacement and the right programming gear.

Can a new fob fix it if my old one was locked inside the car at a US-13 stop?

Sometimes, but it depends on whether you still have a working fob or just need access back into the car. If the only fob is inside and you've locked yourself out, opening the vehicle comes first. If the fob is gone or unusable, we can create and pair a replacement instead of just getting back in and leaving the problem unsolved.

Should I get a replacement remote or a full new fob for my car?

That depends on the vehicle. Some cars use a separate remote and key setup, while others use an integrated fob that handles both security and entry functions. If your system needs a transponder or smart fob, a plain remote won't do the job. We match the replacement to what your vehicle actually uses, not just what looks close.

What if I lost every key and fob, not just the remote part?

That's still a new fob creation job, but it can take more steps. On some vehicles we can generate a replacement and pair it after accessing the car and its security system. On others, the process is tighter and may require additional proof of ownership and model-specific programming. If the car's system won't accept a fresh fob on site, we'll say so plainly.
