Can you make a spare car key if I still have the original key in Cecilton?

Yes. If the original key is working, we can usually duplicate it and, when needed, program the new one to the vehicle. That applies to many cars, trucks, and SUVs. The exact process depends on whether your key is a plain cut key, a transponder key, or a smart key system.

What should I have ready before you come out to duplicate my car key?

Have the vehicle available, along with proof that it's yours. If you have the original key, bring that too. It also helps to know the year, make, and model. Those details let us bring the right blanks and programming gear so we can get the spare made on site when the vehicle allows it.

What if my only key is locked inside the truck near the Sassafras River?

That happens. If the key is locked in the vehicle, we can work on getting access and then figure out the best path for duplication or replacement. Whether we can make a spare right there depends on the vehicle and key type, but we can still handle the lockout first and then move to the key work.

Should I get a plain spare key or a programmed one for my vehicle in Cecilton?

If your vehicle uses a transponder or smart key, a plain cut key may not start it. In that case, a programmed spare is the better choice. For older vehicles with a simple mechanical key, a cut duplicate may be all you need. The right answer depends on the security system in your car or truck.

Can every vehicle in Cecilton be duplicated on site?

Not every one. Many can, but some newer models or worn keys need extra steps, and a few vehicles require dealer-level procedures we can't complete in the field. If your key is damaged, the ignition is worn, or the vehicle has a high-security system, we'll tell you what can and can't be done before any work starts.
