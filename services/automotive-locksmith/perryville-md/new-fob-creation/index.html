New Fob Creation in Perryville - common questions

How does new fob creation work if I don't have a working one left?

We identify the vehicle, source the correct fob, and program it to the car from scratch. If your system needs security access or special programming steps, we handle that as part of the process. The key point is that you don't need an existing working remote for this service to make sense.

What should I have ready when you come to my car in Perryville?

Have the vehicle available, your ID, and any proof that shows you're allowed to have the car serviced. If you have the old fob, even if it seems dead, bring it along. The vehicle's year, make, and model matter too, since the wrong remote can look similar but won't program correctly.

Could this be just a dead battery instead of needing a new fob?

Yes, sometimes. If the doors still respond sometimes or the car recognizes the fob intermittently, a battery or worn contact can be the real issue. If nothing happens at all and there's no backup fob, then the problem may be deeper. We sort out the symptoms before moving ahead.

Should I replace the shell or get a whole new fob for my car?

A shell swap only helps when the electronics still work. If the buttons are worn but the remote is still recognized, that can be enough. If the electronics are missing, damaged, or never paired correctly, a full new fob is the better path. The car's response tells us which option fits.

Can you make a fob if my car is a newer push-button model?

Often, yes, but newer systems can be more security-sensitive and may require specific programming steps. The exact answer depends on the vehicle. If the car uses a smart key system, we verify compatibility first and then build the new fob setup around that system rather than guessing.
