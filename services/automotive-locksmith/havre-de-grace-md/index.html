Automotive locksmith in Havre de Grace - common questions

How do you handle a car key replacement when I don't have the original in Havre de Grace?

We identify the vehicle, cut a new key to match, and program it if the car uses a chip or fob system. For many vehicles, the original key isn't required. We just need the right vehicle information and access to the car. Some models are simpler than others, especially older ones with a standard metal key.

What should I have ready before you come out for a lost car key?

Have the vehicle make, model, and year ready, plus proof that the car is yours. If you know whether it uses a transponder key, remote fob, or push-button start, that helps us bring the right equipment. It also helps if the car is in a safe spot where we can work without getting in the way of traffic or parking.

Is it a bad idea to keep trying a key that sticks in the ignition?

Yes, forcing it can make the problem worse. A sticky key or ignition can mean wear on the key blade, damage inside the cylinder, or debris in the lock. If you keep twisting against resistance, you can end up with a broken key or a bigger ignition repair. It's better to stop and have it checked.

Should I replace my fob or just try reprogramming it?

If the fob still has a sound shell, good buttons, and a working battery, reprogramming may be enough. If it's cracked, water-damaged, or the buttons are worn out, replacement is usually the better choice. Some vehicles also keep more than one fault in the system, so we look at the whole setup before deciding.

Can you help if my key broke off inside the ignition on an older car in Havre de Grace?

Yes. We can remove the broken piece and then check whether the key, the cylinder, or both need attention. Older cars often have more wear in the ignition, so a clean extraction alone may not solve the whole issue. If the lock is damaged, we can repair or replace it so the next key works properly.
