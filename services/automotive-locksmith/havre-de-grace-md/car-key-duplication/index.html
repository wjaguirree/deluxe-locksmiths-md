Car Key Duplication in Havre de Grace - common questions

How do you make a spare car key for a vehicle in Havre de Grace?

We start by identifying the key type and the vehicle's system. Some keys are simple cuts, while others need a chip or remote programmed to the car. Once we know which kind you have, we cut the replacement and set it up so it matches the vehicle, not just the lock cylinder.

What should I have ready before I ask for a car key copy?

Bring the vehicle if possible, along with any working key you still have. It also helps to know the year, make, and model. If the key has buttons or a chip, keep the original with you, even if it is worn. That gives us the best chance of matching the right key.

What if my key opens the door but the car still will not start?

That usually means the issue is not the cut alone. The blade may be right, but the chip in the key may not be programmed, may be damaged, or may be missing altogether. In that case, a plain copy will not solve it. We need to match the vehicle's electronic side as well.

Should I copy my worn key or the spare I hardly use?

Use the least worn working key you have. A key that has been used for years can make a weak copy because the cuts are already rounded off. If the spare is cleaner, it gives a better pattern. When the original is too worn, we may need to work from the vehicle or key code instead.

Can you help if I locked my keys in the car near Tydings Park?

Yes, that situation comes up. The fix depends on whether the key is inside the car, broken in the lock, or simply missing. If the key is locked inside, the goal is to get you back in without damaging the vehicle, then deal with duplication or programming if you need a spare afterward.
