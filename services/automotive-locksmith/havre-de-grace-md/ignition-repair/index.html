Ignition Repair in Havre de Grace - common questions

Why does my key turn hard in the ignition of my car in Havre de Grace?

That usually means the ignition cylinder is worn, dirty, or damaged. The tiny internal parts no longer line up smoothly with the key cuts. A bent key can add to the problem. We can inspect the cylinder, check the key, and see whether the repair is in the lock itself or in the ignition switch behind it.

What should I have ready before you come out for an ignition repair?

Have the vehicle make, model, and year ready, along with proof you're allowed to have the car serviced. If you still have the key that starts it, bring that too, even if it's sticking. If the key is locked inside or broken in the ignition, say so up front so the right tools and parts are brought along.

Can a bad ignition cylinder leave me stuck in downtown Havre de Grace after I park?

Yes. A worn cylinder can trap the key, keep the steering from releasing, or stop the key from turning far enough to shut the car down cleanly. If the lock is already binding, don't force it. Forcing it can snap the key or damage the column and make the repair more involved.

Do you replace the whole ignition, or just the damaged part?

It depends on what failed. Some jobs only need the cylinder rebuilt or replaced. Others need the ignition switch, linkage, or housing checked as well. We try to repair the part that failed without replacing more than necessary, but we won't skip a damaged component just because it still kind of works.

What if my keys are locked in the car at Tydings Park and the ignition is also acting up?

That can be two separate problems in the same call. We can open the vehicle, then inspect the ignition to see why the key was sticking or why it was hard to remove. If the key pattern or cylinder wear is the real issue, we'll explain what needs repair before you drive it again.
