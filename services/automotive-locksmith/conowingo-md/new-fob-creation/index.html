New Fob Creation in Conowingo - common questions

If I have no working fob at all, what do you do first?

We identify the vehicle, confirm the keyless system it uses, and source a new fob that matches it. Then we program that fob to the car from scratch. If the vehicle has other issues, like a dead battery or a problem with the immobilizer, we sort that out before finishing the programming.

What should I have ready when you come out to make a new fob in Conowingo?

Have the vehicle, proof you can authorize service on it, and any old key parts you still have, even if the fob itself is gone. If the car has been sitting, a healthy battery helps. The more exact the vehicle information is, the smoother the job goes.

What if I only lost one fob and the spare is at home?

That is still a good time to make a new one. A spare matters because if the last working fob fails later, you are stuck. We can create and program a new fob so you have a backup instead of relying on one device that could quit at the wrong time.

Can a dead battery in the fob be mistaken for needing a brand-new one?

Yes, and that happens a lot. If the shell is intact and the fob still communicates after battery replacement, you may not need a full replacement. If the electronics are damaged or the car no longer recognizes it, a new fob is the proper fix.

What if the car is locked and the only fob is inside near the river or at a parking spot by the dam?

We can still handle it if the vehicle can be accessed and the right programming steps are available for that model. The exact approach depends on the car. Some systems let us make a new fob right there; others need more setup before the new device will pair correctly.
