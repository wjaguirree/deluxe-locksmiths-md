Automotive locksmith in Glasgow - common questions

My car key will unlock the doors in Glasgow, but the car won't start. What does that usually mean?

That often points to a transponder or programming issue, not the metal key shape itself. The chip inside the key may not be communicating with the vehicle, or the key may be worn enough that it only works partway. We check both the key and the ignition side before replacing anything.

What should I have ready before you come out for a car key problem near Route 896?

Have the vehicle make, model, and year ready, along with proof that the car is yours. If you still have a working key or fob, bring that too. If the problem is a spare key, fob, or ignition issue, that extra key helps us narrow down what needs to be repaired or programmed.

I'm worried the ignition is bad, but the key also feels worn. How do you tell the difference?

We test how the key fits, turns, and releases in the cylinder. If a fresh key works better than the old one, the old key may be worn. If every key binds or won't turn smoothly, the ignition is more likely the issue. Sometimes both are worn, especially on older vehicles with a lot of use.

Should I get a duplicate key or a new fob made for my Glasgow car?

If you already have one working key, duplication is often the simplest path. If all keys are lost, damaged, or the vehicle uses a smart fob system, a new fob or key creation may be needed instead. The right choice depends on whether the car still recognizes any working key data.

Can you help if a key broke off in the ignition and the car is stuck in place?

Yes. Key extraction from the ignition is a separate job, and we can handle that on site. After the broken piece is removed, we check whether the cylinder still operates normally. If the damage is inside the ignition, a repair or replacement may be the better next step.
