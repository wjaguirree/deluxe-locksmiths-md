Ignition Repair in Delaware City - common questions

How do you decide whether my ignition in Delaware City needs repair, rekeying, or replacement?

We start with how the key turns and what the cylinder is doing. If the key is worn but the parts are still usable, rekeying may be enough. If the cylinder is sticking or catching, repair can sometimes restore it. If the internal wear is too far gone or the parts are damaged, replacement is the cleaner fix.

What should I have ready if my ignition is sticking at a parking lot in Delaware City?

Have the vehicle make, model, year, and proof that it's yours. If you have a spare key, bring that too, since it can help show whether the problem is the key or the ignition. If the steering wheel is locked or the shifter won't move, let us know that when you call so we can plan the work correctly.

Can a worn key damage the ignition cylinder in my car?

Yes. A key that's rounded off or bent can wear the cylinder's tumblers over time and make the lock feel rough. If we catch it early, rekeying or a cylinder repair may be enough. If the damage has gone on too long, the cylinder may need replacement to keep the key from binding again.

If my ignition is acting up, why wouldn't you just replace it right away?

Because replacement isn't always the best first step. If the issue is only wear in the key or a small internal problem, repair or rekeying can solve it without changing more parts than necessary. We only recommend replacement when the damage is too severe, the lock is unreliable, or the existing parts can't be restored safely.

My truck key is locked inside near the ferry landing and the ignition is also hard to turn. Can both problems be handled together?

Yes. We can address the lockout and then look at the ignition issue so you're not left with the same problem later. If the key was part of what caused the ignition wear, we'll check whether the cylinder should be repaired, rekeyed, or replaced after we get access to the truck.
