Automotive locksmith in Delaware City - common questions

Can you make a replacement car key for my Delaware City vehicle if I don't have the original?

Yes, in many cases we can create a replacement from the vehicle itself. The make, model, and year decide what's possible, especially with transponder and smart keys. We'll also need the vehicle information and proof it's yours before we start. If there's an existing ignition problem, that can change the plan.

What should I have ready if my key is locked in the car near the ferry landing?

Have your registration and ID ready, and if there's a spare key anywhere, let us know. Tell us whether the key is visible, in the trunk, or trapped in the cabin. Don't try to pry a window or force the lock. Clear space around the vehicle so we can work without extra damage or delays.

My key turns but the ignition feels rough. Should I keep trying it?

No. Forcing a rough ignition can break the key off or damage the cylinder further. If it's turning hard, sticking, or not releasing, stop using it and have us look at it. Sometimes the key is worn; sometimes the ignition itself is failing. We can sort out which part needs attention.

Should I choose a duplicate key, a new fob, or a full replacement for my truck?

If you already have a working key, duplication is usually the simplest backup option. If the fob is dead, missing, or damaged, new fob creation or programming may be the better route. If the original is gone, we may need to build a replacement from the vehicle. The right choice depends on what still works.

What if my car key broke off in the ignition and half of it is still stuck inside?

Leave it alone and don't put another key in behind it. That can wedge the broken piece deeper. Keep both halves if you have them, because they can help us match the cuts and condition of the key. We can handle extraction and then decide whether the ignition, the key, or both need attention.
