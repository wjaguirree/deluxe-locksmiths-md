Ignition Replacement in Bel Air - common questions

How does ignition replacement work if my car is stuck where it sits in Bel Air?

We come to the vehicle and inspect the ignition, key, and steering lock setup on site. If the car can be safely worked on where it's parked, we remove the failed part, install the replacement, and match it to the existing key when the vehicle allows that. If there's more than one fault, we'll explain what we find before moving ahead.

What should I have ready before you replace my ignition switch?

Have the vehicle accessible, the registration if you have it, and any key or fob you normally use with that car. If the steering wheel is locked, the shifter won't move, or the battery is weak, tell us that when you call. Those details help us bring the right parts and understand whether the problem is the ignition itself or something connected to it.

Is there a chance the key I already have won't work with the new ignition?

Sometimes the new ignition can be keyed to your existing key, and sometimes it can't, depending on the vehicle and the damage we find. If the key is badly worn or the lock parts are too damaged, we'll say so upfront. We don't guess on that part, because forcing a worn key into a tired cylinder usually creates another problem later.

Should I replace the ignition or try to repair the old one?

If the cylinder is worn, the switch is failing, or the key no longer turns cleanly, replacement is often the better route. A repair may not hold if the internal parts are already damaged. We check the condition of the parts before we recommend anything, and we'll tell you if the rest of the system still has usable life or if replacement is the cleaner fix.

My car has a push-button start. Can you still help if it won't start in a Bel Air parking lot?

In some cases, yes, but push-button systems are not the same as a standard ignition cylinder. The issue could be the fob, the start system, the brake interlock, or another electrical problem. We can look at the vehicle and tell you whether the fix is in our lane or whether it needs a different kind of repair.
