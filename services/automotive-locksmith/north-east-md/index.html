Automotive locksmith in North East - common questions

My car won't recognize the fob in North East. Can you still help if the key is inside the vehicle?

Yes. We can diagnose whether it's a dead fob battery, a programming issue, or a problem with the vehicle's receiver. If the key is locked inside, we can also handle the lockout and then check whether the fob still needs programming or replacement. That keeps the car from being taken somewhere else just to sort out the next step.

What should I have ready before you work on my car key or ignition?

Have the vehicle's make, model, and year ready, along with proof that you can authorize the work. If you have any working spare key, bring that too. If the problem started after the key broke, the fob stopped acting right, or the ignition is sticking, tell us what happened so we can bring the right tools and parts.

Can a worn key damage my ignition if I keep using it near the waterfront or around town?

It can. A key that's bent, heavily worn, or starting to chip can make the ignition harder to turn and may leave part of the key stuck inside. Salt air and everyday wear can make an already tired key or cylinder act up sooner. If you notice the key binding, don't force it.

Should I replace my fob or just have a new one programmed?

That depends on the condition of the fob and the vehicle system. If the housing is broken, buttons are failing, or the electronics are damaged, a new fob may make more sense. If the fob itself is fine and just needs to be recognized by the car, programming may be enough. We can tell you which path fits the setup.

My key snapped off in the ignition on an older truck. Is that something you can fix on site?

Often, yes. We can extract the broken key from the ignition and check whether the cylinder is still usable. If the ignition is worn out or damaged from the break, we may need to repair or replace it. Older trucks and work vehicles are common candidates for this kind of repair because the parts see a lot of use.
