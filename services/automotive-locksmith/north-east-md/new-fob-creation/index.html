How does new fob creation work when I don't have a working one left in North East?

What should I have ready before I call about a lost car fob?

Can a new fob still be made if the last one was lost near the river or on a boat?

Yes, in many cases it can. What matters is the vehicle information and whether the car itself is accessible. If the old fob is gone for good, we can still source a new one and program it to the car. If the battery is weak or the vehicle has other issues, that may need to be addressed first.
