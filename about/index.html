About Deluxe Locksmith

A locksmith business covering Northeast Maryland & Delaware, working out of Elkton, MD.

Call (443) 545-2775 Request service

Deluxe Locksmith is a locksmith business working out of 121 W Main St, Elkton, MD 21921, covering Northeast Maryland & Delaware. We handle automotive, residential, commercial and emergency work - car keys and fobs, rekeys and lock upgrades, storefront and office hardware, and lockouts of every kind.

Where we work

Our service area is four counties: Cecil County, MD, Harford County, MD, New Castle County, DE, Kent County, DE. That takes in 38 towns, from Elkton and the I-95 corridor through Bel Air, Aberdeen and Havre de Grace, and down the Route 13 spine through Dover, Smyrna and Milford. See the full list of towns.

How we work

Almost everything is done where you are - at the kerb, at the door, at the storefront. We carry what the job needs rather than making you bring the problem to us. Our shop address is a real address, but in-store visits are by appointment only, so please call before travelling to us.

We do not quote prices or arrival windows over a web page, and we would be suspicious of anyone who did. Too much depends on what the lock actually is, what has already been tried and where you are. Call, describe what happened, and you will get a straight answer from someone who does this work.

The bench

Some work is better done at a bench than on a doorstep - servicing a lock properly, cutting a set of keys to a restricted profile, putting together the cylinders for a master key system. That is what the shop is for.

Hours

We are open Sunday through Thursday from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM, Friday from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and closed on Saturdays.
