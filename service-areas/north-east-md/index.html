Locksmith in North East - common questions

Can you rekey my house after I move into a place in North East?

Yes. Rekeying is a common choice after a move, after a tenant leaves, or when you don't know how many copies of the old key are out there. The existing locks stay in place, but the old keys stop working. It's a practical way to reset access without replacing every lock on the door.

What should I have ready if my car key is lost around North East?

Have the vehicle location, the make, model, and year, and a way to show you're the owner or have permission to have the car serviced. If there's a spare key or key fob at home, that helps too. If the car is in a parking lot, driveway, or marina area, make sure it's safe and accessible before the work starts.

My shop door key sticks sometimes. Is that a lock problem or a key problem?

It can be either one, or both. A worn key may work in one direction and fail in another, while a lock that's dirty, misaligned, or damaged can give the same symptom. In a business setting, it's worth checking before the key breaks off or the door stops latching the way it should.

Should I rekey or replace the lock on my North East rental?

If the hardware is in good shape, rekeying is often enough after a tenant change. If the lock is loose, corroded, damaged, or doesn't match the door well anymore, replacement makes more sense. The right choice depends on the condition of the hardware and how much control you want over access going forward.

What if the key broke off in the lock on an older house near the water?

That can happen when a key is worn or a lock is starting to seize from age or exposure. Don't force it deeper. The key fragment usually needs to be removed first, then the lock should be checked for wear or alignment issues. On older homes, it's common to find that the lock needs more than just the broken piece cleared out.
