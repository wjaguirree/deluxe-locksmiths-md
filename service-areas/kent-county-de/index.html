Towns we serve

Kent County, DE

Dover, DE

Dover is Delaware's capital and by far the largest city we cover — Legislative Hall and the state offices around The Green, Delaware State University, Dover Air Force Base with its constant rotation of families arriving on orders, and the Monster Mile at Dover Motor Speedway drawing enormous crowds on race weekends.

Locksmith in Dover →

Smyrna, DE

Smyrna straddles the top of Kent County where Route 1 and US-13 run north toward the canal, and it has grown quickly — new subdivisions ringing a genuinely historic downtown of brick storefronts, the Smyrna Opera House and streets of old frame houses.

Locksmith in Smyrna →

Milford, DE

Milford sits astride the Mispillion River with one foot in Kent County and one in Sussex, and it has done a genuinely good job of turning the river into the center of town — the Riverwalk, the restored downtown along Walnut Street, the shipbuilding history along the banks.

Locksmith in Milford →

Camden, DE

Camden runs directly into Dover from the south along Route 13, and the two towns effectively share a commercial corridor — the shopping centers, car dealers and restaurants that serve most of central Kent County.

Locksmith in Camden →

Harrington, DE

Harrington is a Route 13 railroad town that swells enormously for one week every July, when the Delaware State Fair takes over the fairgrounds and the whole of central Delaware drives in.

Locksmith in Harrington →

Felton, DE

Felton is a small Route 13 town in southern Kent County, close to Killens Pond State Park and surrounded by some of the most productive farmland in Delaware.

Locksmith in Felton →

Clayton, DE

Clayton sits in the northwest corner of Kent County, right against the Maryland line, and it was built by the railroad — the tracks still run straight through the middle of town.

Locksmith in Clayton →

Wyoming, DE

Wyoming is a small, tidy town pressed right up against Camden south of Dover, well known locally for its peach-growing past and the Peach Festival that still marks the summer.

How it works

Getting help is straightforward

  1. Get in touch

    Ring us or send the details through the site. Either reaches the same person and gets the same answer.

  2. We scope the job

    What kind of lock, what kind of door, what has already been attempted. Three questions, and we know what to bring.

  3. We arrive equipped

    The right blanks, the right programmer, the right hardware. Turning up and then going away again helps nobody.

  4. You check it yourself

    Try the key. Work the lock. Shut the door. If anything is not right we sort it there and then.
