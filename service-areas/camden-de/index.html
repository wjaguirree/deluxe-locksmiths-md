Locksmith in Camden - common questions

If my front door in Camden sticks, should I keep trying the key or stop?

Stop once the key starts to bind or flex. Keeping pressure on it can snap the key or damage the cylinder. If the door is also rubbing at the frame, the issue may be alignment rather than the lock itself. We can inspect the hardware and figure out whether it needs repair, rekeying, or replacement.

What should I have ready if I'm locked out of my car near Camden-Wyoming?

Have the vehicle make, model, and year ready, plus proof that it's your car if we need to verify ownership. If there's a spare key with someone nearby, mention that too. It also helps to tell us whether the key is lost, locked inside, broken, or not turning in the ignition.

Is it a bad idea to spray oil into a lock on Main Street if it feels rough?

Usually, yes. Random oil or household sprays can make a lock collect grit and work worse later. If a lock is stiff, the issue may be wear, dirt, or a damaged key. We can clean it properly, check the key cut, and see whether the cylinder needs repair or replacement.

Should I rekey my house in Camden after moving in, or replace the locks?

If the locks are in good shape, rekeying is often the cleaner choice because the old keys stop working and the hardware stays in place. Replacement makes more sense if the lock is worn, damaged, or not a style you want to keep. We can look at each door and talk through the options.

Can you help if my mailbox, gate, or utility lock in Camden isn't the usual house type?

Yes, as long as the lock is accessible and the work is within the type of service we handle. Small specialty locks can be different from a front door, so it helps to describe the item and send a clear photo if you can. Some units can be repaired; others are better replaced.
