Locksmith in Dover - common questions

My front door lock turns, but the bolt doesn't seem to catch. What should I check first in Dover?

Start with the door itself. If it's rubbing, sagging, or swollen, the lock may be fine and the strike is just out of alignment. Check whether the bolt lines up with the frame and whether the door closes fully without pressure. If the key turns but nothing engages, stop using force and have the hardware inspected.

What should I gather before calling for a house lockout service?

Have your ID ready if you can reach it, and be prepared to describe the lock type, the door material, and whether there's a spare key nearby. If it's a shared building or rental, tell the locksmith that up front. That helps confirm access and avoid extra back-and-forth at the door.

I'm worried the key broke inside the lock. What should I not do?

Don't keep turning the key or try to dig the piece out with a knife, paper clip, or drill. That usually pushes the fragment deeper or scratches the cylinder. Leave both ends alone and keep the door closed if you still can. A locksmith can remove the broken piece and tell you whether the lock can be saved.

Should I rekey the locks or replace them after moving into a house in Dover?

If the hardware is in good shape, rekeying is often the cleaner option because the old keys stop working without changing the whole lock. Replace the lock if it's worn, damaged, mismatched, or missing parts. If you want every exterior door to match on one key, say that when you call so the setup can be planned that way.

My car key fob stopped working in a parking lot. Is it always a locksmith issue?

Not always. A dead fob battery or a weak vehicle battery can cause the same symptoms as a lost or failed key. Check for any warning lights and try a spare if you have one. If the spare works, that points to the key or fob itself. If neither works, a locksmith can help sort out the next step.
