How it works

What happens after you ring us

  1. Get in touch

    Ring us or send the details through the site. Either reaches the same person and gets the same answer.

  2. We scope the job

    What kind of lock, what kind of door, what has already been attempted. Three questions, and we know what to bring.

  3. We arrive equipped

    The right blanks, the right programmer, the right hardware. Turning up and then going away again helps nobody.

  4. You check it yourself

    Try the key. Work the lock. Shut the door. If anything is not right we sort it there and then.

Questions people ask us

What areas do you cover?

We cover Cecil County, MD, Harford County, MD, New Castle County, DE, Kent County, DE - 38 towns in total. The counties are the boundary, so if your town is not listed it may still be inside the area.

Do you come to me, or do I come to you?

We come to you for almost everything. Our address is a working shop, but in-store visits are by appointment only - please call before travelling to us.

Why are there no figures quoted on the site?

Because an honest number is not possible in advance. What a job takes depends on the exact lock or vehicle, what has already been tried and where you are. Call, describe it, and we will tell you what is involved.

When are you open?

Sunday through Thursday, 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Closed Saturdays.

Can you make a car key if I have lost every copy?

Yes. That is a normal job rather than an unusual one - see car key replacement and lost car key replacement.

I just bought a house. What should I do first?

Rekey it. You have no way of knowing how many copies of that key exist or who has them. See lock rekeying.
