How to Program a Key Fob and When to Call a Pro

Learn how to program a key fob: check the battery, try your owner's manual steps, and know when your fob needs a mobile locksmith's programming equipment.

How to program a key fob depends on what's in your driveway. Some cars take a new remote in about two minutes with the owner's manual open on your lap, and some won't budge without a diagnostic tool plugged into the dash. Most calls we get about this start with somebody who tried the first kind of car's steps on the second kind of car.

So here's how we'd handle it. Battery. Manual. Then us.

Key Takeaways

  • A weak battery looks exactly like a fob that lost its pairing. AAA says the swap can be done without a trip to the dealership.
  • Your manual has the steps for your car. A forum post has the steps for somebody else's.
  • Door buttons and engine start are different jobs, and the second is the hard one.
  • No working key left means you're past DIY.

What does programming a key fob mean?

Programming a key fob means teaching your car to accept that fob's signal. That's it. But a fob isn't one thing. It may only work the door locks. It may also send the push-to-start signal. And it may carry a chip the immobilizer wants to see before the engine will run.

Door buttons are the easy bit. The chip is what makes a spare key a job for someone with equipment.

What is the Fob Ladder?

We call it the Fob Ladder, and it's just the order we check things. Bottom rung first. You only climb when the one below fails you.

RungWhat to doWho does it
1. BatteryReplace the coin battery and test every buttonYou
2. Owner's manualFollow the pairing steps listed for your exact vehicleYou
3. Programming equipmentPair the fob and chip to the car with a diagnostic toolLocksmith
4. Key cutting and chip enrollmentCut the blade and enroll a new chip when no working key existsLocksmith

People love rung three. It feels like the real fix. But a coin battery swap takes a few minutes, and it cures a surprising share of dead fobs. Our key fob not working checklist has the details.

Can you program a key fob yourself?

Maybe. Some cars want you in the driver's seat, ignition turned to a certain spot, one button pressed fast. Done. Other cars shut that option off completely, and only a dealer or locksmith tool gets in. Check the manual. If there's a remote section with steps, you've got the first kind of car. If the manual says nothing, you've got the second.

The trouble with online step lists is that they're written for one model year, and the next year's car ignores them. A wrong sequence can do worse than nothing, leaving the car in a programming mode that sits there until it times out. Not fun at 6 a.m. with a commute.

When does a key fob need a locksmith?

Fresh battery, no luck. Manual silent on pairing. Only key gone. Any of those and you're on rung three, where we bring the equipment to wherever the car is parked.

The fob that starts the engine always lands here, because the chip has to be enrolled in the immobilizer. So does the fob you ordered online. If it was never cut and programmed for your car, it's a plastic case, and some are locked to another vehicle for good. And if every key is gone, what to do when you lose your only car key comes before any shopping.

We handle key fob programming and new fob creation at the vehicle. A few cars, mostly newer European ones, are dealer-only, and we'd rather tell you that on the phone than waste your evening.

What happens when we program a fob at your car?

You walk us to the car. We confirm the year, make and model, check the new fob against it, and pair it. If the engine needs a chip, we enroll that too. Then comes the part we won't skip: door locks, trunk, remote start if you've got it, and a full engine start. A fob that opens the doors but won't start the car is half done, and we don't leave half done. Nothing gets drilled or forced, and your other keys keep working.

What should you ask before you book?

The same things you'd ask anyone. The Maryland Department of Labor says all locksmiths in Maryland are required to be licensed, and it advises customers to request an estimate for the service call and the work to be performed. It also says the invoice should list the VIN for automobile locks serviced (Maryland Locksmith Consumer Advisory).

So get the total before work starts, since our pricing is quoted per job. Ask if the engine start gets tested. Ask for the VIN on the invoice. And have your year, make and model ready, plus any working key.

Frequently asked questions

How do I program a key fob myself?

Open the owner's manual and find the remote pairing section. Steps there? Follow them exactly. No section? The car wants a tool, and that means a locksmith or the dealer.

How do I reprogram a key fob after a battery change?

Most of the time you don't. A battery swap doesn't wipe the pairing on most fobs. If it quit afterward, look at the battery's direction and the contacts first.

Can a locksmith program a key fob?

Yes, at your vehicle. No tow and no dealer trip. A few makes are dealer-only and we'll tell you before we drive out.

Where can I get a key fob programmed near me?

A dealer, or a locksmith who comes to you. We program fobs at your car across Elkton and the rest of our Maryland and Delaware service area.

Will programming a new fob stop my old one from working?

It shouldn't. A normal pairing adds a fob and leaves the others alone. Ask the technician to check your current key before they go anyway.
